# Rule 1.3 — Use Approved Words Only with Their Approved Meanings

> **Source:** Adapted from ASD-STE100 Issue 9, Rule 1.3

## Original Rule

**Rule 1.3** Use approved words only with their approved meanings.

Each approved word in the dictionary has a specified approved meaning. Some of these words can have more restricted meanings compared with their meanings in standard English. Always use the approved words only with their approved meanings.

Examples:

The approved meaning of the verb "follow" is "come after, go after."

> **STE:** Do the procedures that follow:

You cannot use the verb "follow" with other meanings that are not approved.

In this sentence, always use "obey" with the approved meaning "to do that which the procedures or instructions tell you."

## STE-Code Adaptation

**Rule 1.3** Use approved words only with their approved meanings.

Each approved word in the controlled terminology has a specified approved meaning. Some of these words can have more restricted meanings compared with their meanings in standard English. Always use the approved words only with their approved meanings.

The approved meaning of the verb "call" is "to invoke a function, method, or subroutine." You cannot use "call" with other meanings such as "to name" or "to telephone."

The approved meaning of the verb "return" is "to send a value back from a function or method to its caller." You cannot use "return" with the meaning "to go back to a previous state" unless paired with a technical noun that makes the meaning clear.

When an approved word has only one approved meaning in the controlled terminology, do not use it with any other meaning from standard English. If you need to express a different meaning, find an alternative approved word or use a different sentence construction.

### Examples

> **Non-STE:** Call the variable userCount and then return to the previous screen.
> **STE:** Name the variable userCount and then go back to the previous screen.

The approved meaning of "call" is "to invoke a function, method, or subroutine." In the non-STE example, "call" is used with the meaning "to name," which is not approved. The STE version uses the approved verb "name."

The approved meaning of "return" is "to send a value back from a function or method to its caller." In the non-STE example, "return" is used to mean "go back," which is not an approved meaning. The STE version uses "go back."

> **Non-STE:** Follow the steps in the API documentation.
> **STE:** Obey the instructions in the API documentation.

The approved meaning of "follow" is "come after, go after." In the non-STE example, "follow" is used with the meaning "to act in accordance with." The STE version uses the approved verb "obey" with the approved meaning "to do that which the procedures or instructions tell you."
