## Approved Words

#### PULL (v)

- (PULLS, PULLED, PULLED) To use a force on something to move it toward the source of the force
- Use this word together with a preposition or an adverb to show direction.
- **STE:** PULL THE CABLE DOWN AND AWAY FROM THE BRACKET.

#### PUSH (v)

- (PUSHES, PUSHED, PUSHED) To apply a force to something to move it away from the source of the force
- Use this word together with a preposition or an adverb to show direction.
- **STE:** THE SPRING UNIT PUSHES AGAINST THE BRAKE PEDAL.

#### PUT (v)

- To place something in a position or condition
- No other verb forms.
- **STE:** PUT THE ADAPTER IN POSITION AGAINST ITS SUPPORT.
- **STE:** PUT THE LEVER BACK TO ITS INITIAL POSITION.

#### PUT ON (v)

- (PUTS ON, PUT ON, PUT ON) To cover your skin or face with clothing, or other items that give protection
- No other verb forms.
- **STE:** PUT ON SAFETY GOGGLES AND PROTECTIVE CLOTHING.

#### QUALITY (n)

- Condition, property, or type
- **STE:** THIS MEDICAL EQUIPMENT MUST BE OF GOOD QUALITY.

#### QUANTITY (n)

- A specified amount or number
- **STE:** APPLY A SMALL QUANTITY OF LUBRICATING OIL ON THE THREADS OF THE BOLTS.
- For an amount that you can count, use NUMBER (n).
- **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT YOU HAVE THE CORRECT NUMBER OF SHIMS AVAILABLE.

#### QUICKLY (adv)

- In a fast manner
- **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE PRESSURE DECREASES QUICKLY.

## Unapproved Words

#### providing (that) (conj)

- **Approved alternative:** IF (conj)
- **STE:** IF A NEW PART IS INSTALLED, YOU CAN CONTINUE THE OPERATION OF THE MACHINE.
- **Non-STE:** Providing that a new part is installed, you can continue the operation of the machine.

#### proximity (n)

- **Approved alternative:** NEAR (prep)
- **STE:** DO NOT DO MAINTENANCE WORK NEAR AN ENGINE THAT IS IN OPERATION.
- **Non-STE:** Do not do maintenance work in proximity to a running engine.

#### pump (v)

- **Approved alternative:** REMOVE (v) (WITH A PUMP [TN])
- **STE:** OPEN THE VALVE. THEN, REMOVE THE OIL FROM THE TANK WITH AN APPLICABLE PUMP.
- **Non-STE:** Open the valve and then pump oil from the tank.
- **Approved alternative:** MOVE (v) (WITH A PUMP [TN])
- **STE:** WHEN THE VALVE IS OPEN, MOVE OIL FROM THE TANK TO THE CIRCUIT LINE WITH AN APPLICABLE PUMP.
- **Non-STE:** When the valve is open, pump oil from the tank to the circuit lines.

#### puncture (v)

- **Approved alternative:** HOLE (n)
- **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT YOU DO NOT MAKE A HOLE IN THE BLADDER.
- **Non-STE:** Take care not to puncture the bladder.

#### purge (v)

- **Approved alternative:** REMOVE (v)
- **STE:** REMOVE UNWANTED MATERIAL FROM THE SYSTEM.
- **Non-STE:** Purge the system.

#### purify (v)

- **Approved alternative:** CLEAN (v)
- **STE:** CLEAN THE WATER.
- **Non-STE:** Purify the water.

#### qualified (adj)

- **Approved alternative:** APPROVED (adj)
- **STE:** AN APPROVED PERSON MUST DO THE IMPORTANT CHECKS.
- **Non-STE:** A qualified person must do the important checks.

#### quarterly (adv)

- **Approved alternative:** INTERVAL (n)
- **STE:** SEND REPORTS TO THE AUTHORITIES AT INTERVALS OF THREE MONTHS.
- **Non-STE:** Send reports to the authorities quarterly.

#### questionable (adj)

- **Approved alternative:** NOT SURE
- **STE:** IF YOU ARE NOT SURE OF THEIR CONDITION, REJECT THE PARTS.
- **Non-STE:** Reject the parts that are in a questionable condition.

#### quick (adj)

- **Approved alternative:** QUICKLY (adv)
- **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE ROD DOES NOT MOVE QUICKLY.
- **Non-STE:** Make sure that there is no quick movement of the rod.

#### quit (v)

- **Approved alternative:** GO (v)
- **STE:** BEFORE YOU START THE ENGINE, MAKE SURE THAT ALL PERSONS GO AWAY FROM THE DANGER AREA.
- **Non-STE:** Before you start the engine, make sure that all persons quit the danger area.
- **Approved alternative:** STOP (v)
- **STE:** IF THE RED LIGHT COMES ON, STOP THE REFUEL PROCEDURE.
- **Non-STE:** If the red light comes on, quit the refuel procedure.

#### quote (v)

- **Approved alternative:** GIVE (v)
- **STE:** THE TABLE GIVES THE MAXIMUM TOLERANCE FOR THE HOLE.
- **Non-STE:** The table quotes the maximum tolerance for the hole.
