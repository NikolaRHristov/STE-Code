## Dictionary

#### decay (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** DECREASE (v)

> **STE:** WHEN THE ENGINE SPEED DECREASES TO 500 RPM, STOP THE ENGINE.
> **Non-STE:** When the engine speed decays to 500 rpm, stop the engine.

#### decelerate (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** DECREASE (v), SLOWER (adj)

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE ENGINE SPEED DECREASES TO 500 RPM.
> **Non-STE:** Make sure that the engine decelerates to 500 rpm.

> **STE:** TO MAKE THE CURING PROCESS SLOWER, DECREASE THE HEAT THAT YOU APPLY TO THE COMPOUND.
> **Non-STE:** The curing process of the compound can be decelerated by decreasing the heat applied.

#### decide (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** SELECT (v)

> **STE:** SELECT THE HYDRAULIC SYSTEM THAT YOU WILL PRESSURIZE.
> **Non-STE:** Decide which hydraulic system you will pressurize.

#### DECISION (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** The result after you think about a problem.

> **STE:** IF YOUR DECISION IS NOT TO REPLACE THE UNIT, MONITOR ITS TEMPERATURE FOR A MINIMUM OF 15 CYCLES.

> **STE:** MAKE A DECISION ABOUT WHICH PROCEDURE YOU WILL USE.

#### decontamination (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** REMOVE (v) + CONTAMINATION (TN), CLEAN (v)

> **STE:** REMOVE THE CONTAMINATION FROM THE INSTRUMENTS AND MEDICAL EQUIPMENT.
> **Non-STE:** Do the decontamination of the instruments and medical equipment.

> **STE:** CLEAN THE AIRCRAFT IN AN APPROVED AREA.
> **Non-STE:** The decontamination of the aircraft shall be done in an authorized area.

#### decrease (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** DECREASE (v)

> **STE:** THE PRESSURE MUST DECREASE BY MORE THAN 2 BAR.
> **Non-STE:** There must be a decrease in pressure of more than 2 bar.

#### DECREASE (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To make or become smaller or lower.
- **Forms:** DECREASES, DECREASED, DECREASED

> **STE:** DECREASE THE HEADING INDICATIONS.

#### decrement (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** INCREMENT (n)

> **STE:** TURN THE ADJUSTER COUNTERCLOCKWISE IN INCREMENTS OF 60 DEG.
> **Non-STE:** Turn the adjuster anticlockwise in decrements of 60 deg.

#### DEDICATED (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** That is used only for one purpose.

> **STE:** A DEDICATED TOOL IS NECESSARY FOR THE INSTALLATION OF THE UNIT.

#### DE-ENERGIZE (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To remove electrical power from.
- **Forms:** DE-ENERGIZES, DE-ENERGIZED, DE-ENERGIZED

> **STE:** DE-ENERGIZE THE AC AND DC BUSBARS.

#### DEEP (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** That has a specified depth.
- **Forms:** DEEPER, DEEPEST

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE HOLE IS 20 mm DEEP.

#### DEFECTIVE (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Unserviceable for operation.

> **STE:** REPLACE THE DEFECTIVE BOOSTER PUMP.

#### define (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** CALCULATE (v), GIVE (v), SPECIFIED (adj)

> **STE:** CALCULATE THE QUANTITY OF FUEL THAT IS NECESSARY FOR THE FLIGHT.
> **Non-STE:** Define the amount of fuel required for the flight.

> **STE:** THE BRAKE INDICATOR GIVES THE WEAR ON THE BRAKE SHOE.
> **Non-STE:** The brake indicator defines the wear on the brake shoe.

> **STE:** THE FLAP ANGLES ARE SPECIFIED IN TABLE 1.
> **Non-STE:** Table 1 defines the flap angles.

#### DEFLATE (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To make or become smaller as a result of depressurization.
- **Forms:** DEFLATES, DEFLATED, DEFLATED

> **STE:** DEFLATE THE TIRE TO A PRESSURE OF 2 bar (29 psi).

> **STE:** IF THE WHEEL TEMPERATURE INCREASES TO MORE THAN 177 DEG., THE CORE OF THE FUSIBLE PLUG MELTS AND THE TIRE DEFLATES.

#### deflect (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** MOVE (v)

> **STE:** THE BARS MOVE TO THE RIGHT SIDE.
> **Non-STE:** Bars deflect to the right.

#### deflection (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** MOVEMENT (n), POSITION (n), TRAVEL (n)

> **STE:** REMOVE ALL THE RIGGING PINS AND EQUIPMENT THAT PREVENT FREE MOVEMENT OF THE CONTROL SURFACES.
> **Non-STE:** Remove all rigging pins and equipment preventing free deflection of control surfaces.

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE POSITION IS 23 DEG.
> **Non-STE:** Ensure that deflection is 23 deg.

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE TRAVEL IS 23 DEG.
> **Non-STE:** Ensure that deflection is 23 deg.

#### deformed (adj) — UNAPPROVED

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE TUBES HAVE NO DEFORMATION.

#### DEFUEL (v) — APPROVED

#### de-ice (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** ICE (TN)

#### delay (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** INTERVAL (n), IMMEDIATELY (adv)
