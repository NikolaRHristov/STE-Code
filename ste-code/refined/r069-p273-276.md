## APPROVED Words

#### HYDRAULIC (adj)

- Related to, or operated by, pressurized liquid
  > **STE:** CONNECT THE FITTINGS TO THE HYDRAULIC TEST BENCH.

#### HYDRAULICALLY (adv)

- With hydraulic power
  > **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE LINES ARE HYDRAULICALLY OPERATED.

#### IDENTIFICATION (n)

- Something that identifies an object
  > **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE NAMEPLATE SHOWS THE CORRECT IDENTIFICATION OF THE UNIT.

#### IDENTIFY (v) — IDENTIFIES, IDENTIFIED, IDENTIFIED

- 1. To use a specified code or marking to supply with an identity
  > **STE:** IDENTIFY THE WIRES WITH SLEEVES OF DIFFERENT COLORS.
- 2. To show an identity
  > **STE:** A 406 MHz DIGITAL SIGNAL IDENTIFIES THE EMERGENCY LOCATOR TRANSMITTER AND ITS LOCATION.
- For other meanings, use:
  - **FIND (v)**
    > **STE:** FIND THE DAMAGED AREAS.
    > **Non-STE:** Identify the damaged areas.

#### IF (conj)

- In the event that, on the condition that, in case of
  > **STE:** IF YOU USE A REPLACEMENT FAIRING PLATE, CUT IT UNTIL YOU GET THE CORRECT MATING DIMENSION.

#### IGNORE (v) — IGNORES, IGNORED, IGNORED

- Not to think about something, not to do something about
  > **STE:** IGNORE THE VIBRATION INDICATIONS.
  > **STE:** SELECT "CANCEL" TO IGNORE ALL CHANGES.

#### ILLUMINATE (v) — ILLUMINATES, ILLUMINATED, ILLUMINATED

- To make something visible with an external light source
  > **STE:** THE EMERGENCY LIGHTS COME ON TO ILLUMINATE THE EVACUATION PATH.
- For lights or lamps, use:
  - **COME ON**
    > **STE:** WHEN THE PRESSURE LIGHT COMES ON, READ THE VALUE ON THE PRESSURE GAUGE.
    > **Non-STE:** When the pressure light illuminates, read the value on the pressure gauge.
    > **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE LAMP COMES ON.
    > **Non-STE:** Ensure the lamp illuminates.
  - **BE ON**
    > **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE LAMP IS ON.
    > **Non-STE:** Ensure the lamp is illuminated.

#### IMMEDIATELY (adv)

- Without a gap of time or space
  > **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE EMERGENCY LIGHT IMMEDIATELY GOES BACK TO "OFF."
