## Approved Words

#### RADIAL (adj)

- **Approved meaning:** Along a radius

> **STE:** DO A CHECK FOR RADIAL CUTS, CRACKS, OR SPLITS.

#### RADIALLY (adv)

- **Approved meaning:** Along a radius

> **STE:** THE FAN BLADES ARE INSTALLED RADIALLY.

#### RADIOACTIVE (adj)

- **Approved meaning:** Related to radioactivity

> **STE:** DO A CHECK FOR RADIOACTIVE CONTAMINATION.

#### RANDOM (adj)

- **Approved meaning:** Not regular

> **STE:** DURING THIS PROCEDURE, DO RANDOM CHECKS OF THE TIRE PRESSURE.

#### RANDOMLY (adv)

- **Approved meaning:** Not regularly

> **STE:** DO THESE CHECKS RANDOMLY.

#### RANGE (n)

- **Approved meaning:** The limits within which something operates

> **STE:** THE AUDIO SPECTRUM IS THE FREQUENCY RANGE AT WHICH HUMANS CAN HEAR.

#### RATE (n)

- **Approved meaning:** A measurement of how frequently or how quickly something occurs

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE FLOW RATE IS CORRECT.

#### READ (v)

- **Approved meaning:** To come to know information with the eyes or electronically
- **Forms:** READS, READ, READ

> **STE:** READ THE INDICATORS ON THE CONTROL PANEL.

#### REAR (adj)

- **Approved meaning:** Away from the person who looks or from a reference point

> **STE:** INSTALL THE TWO BOLTS IN THE REAR FITTINGS.

#### REAR (n)

- **Approved meaning:** The rear part or rear surface

> **STE:** THE CIRCUIT BREAKERS ARE INSTALLED ON THE REAR OF THE UNIT.

#### REARWARD (adv)

- **Approved meaning:** In the direction of the rear

> **STE:** PULL THE LEVER REARWARD.

#### RECEIVE (v)

- **Approved meaning:** To get energy, material, or a signal from a different source
- **Forms:** RECEIVES, RECEIVED, RECEIVED

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE CAPTAIN'S BOOMSET RECEIVES THE SIGNALS CORRECTLY.

#### RECESS (n)

- **Approved meaning:** An indentation in a surface

> **STE:** WHEN YOU CLEAN THE SURFACES, REMOVE THE STAINS FROM THE RECESSES.

## Unapproved Words

#### raise (v)

- **Alternative 1:** LIFT (v)

> **STE:** DO NOT LIFT THE COVER MORE THAN THE SPECIFIED CLEARANCE.
> **Non-STE:** Do not raise the cover more than the specified clearance.

- **Alternative 2:** INCREASE (v)

> **STE:** INCREASE THE TEMPERATURE BY 10 DEG.
> **Non-STE:** Raise the temperature 10 DEG.

- **Alternative 3:** RETRACT (v)

> **STE:** RETRACT THE LANDING GEAR.
> **Non-STE:** Raise the landing gear.

#### rapid (adj)

- **Alternative:** FAST (adj)

> **STE:** CHEMICAL AGENTS CAUSE FAST DETERIORATION OF PAINTED SURFACES.
> **Non-STE:** Chemical agents cause rapid deterioration of painted surfaces.

#### rapidly (adv)

- **Alternative:** QUICKLY (adv)

> **STE:** QUICKLY TURN THE HANDLE CLOCKWISE.
> **Non-STE:** Rapidly turn the handle clockwise.

#### re- (prefix)

- **Note:** In general, use the word AGAIN (adv) or BACK (adv) with the basic word to replace words that have "re-" as a prefix.

#### reach (v)

- **Alternative 1:** GET (v)

> **STE:** WHEN YOU GET THE CORRECT PRESSURE, CLOSE THE VALVE.
> **Non-STE:** When the correct pressure is reached, close the valve.

- **Alternative 2:** TOUCH (v)

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE EXTENSION BAR TOUCHES THE TARGET PLATE.
> **Non-STE:** Make sure that the extension bar reaches the target plate.

- **Alternative 3:** BE (v)

> **STE:** WHEN THE FLAPS ARE AT THE 30 DEGREE POSITION, INSTALL THE RIGGING PIN.
> **Non-STE:** When the flaps reach the 30 degree position, install the rigging pin.

#### reactivate (v)

- **Alternative:** ACTIVATE (v)

> **STE:** ACTIVATE THE MODE AGAIN.
> **Non-STE:** Reactivate the mode.

#### reading (n)

- **Alternative:** INDICATION (n)

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE EXHAUST FROM THE GROUND CART DOES NOT CAUSE AN INCORRECT INDICATION.

#### readjust (v)

- **Alternative:** ADJUST (v)

#### ready (adj)
