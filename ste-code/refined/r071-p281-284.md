# ASD-STE100 — Dictionary (I: incomplete – INPUT)

> **Source:** ASD-STE100 Issue 9, January 2025
> **Pages:** 281–284 of 434

## Dictionary

### I

#### IDENTIFY (v) — APPROVED

- > **STE:** A 406 MHz DIGITAL SIGNAL IDENTIFIES THE EMERGENCY LOCATOR TRANSMITTER AND ITS LOCATION.
- > **Non-STE:** A 406 MHz digital signal indicates the emergency locator transmitter and its location.

#### INCORRECT (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Not correct
- **STE:** IF THE TRIM TAB ADJUSTMENT IS INCORRECT, DO STEPS 7 THRU 10.

#### INCORRECTLY (adv) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** In an incorrect manner
- **STE:** IF YOU SEE THE RED MARK, THE PART IS INSTALLED INCORRECTLY.

#### INCREASE (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To make or become larger or higher in value
- **Forms:** INCREASES, INCREASED
- **STE:** INCREASE THE VANE ANGLE TO 90 DEGREES.
- **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE TEMPERATURE INCREASES TO 30 °C.

#### INCREMENT (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** One of a sequence of regular operations done one after the other, to increase or decrease an adjustable quantity
- **STE:** ADJUST THE STOP BOLT IN INCREMENTS OF 5 mm.

#### INDICATION (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Something that is shown
- **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE INDICATION IS CORRECT.

#### INFLATE (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To make or become larger as a result of pressurization by a gas
- **Forms:** INFLATES, INFLATED
- **STE:** INFLATE THE TIRES WITH NITROGEN.
- **STE:** OPERATION OF THE HANDLE RELEASES THE GAS, AND THE SLIDE-RAFT INFLATES.

#### INFORMATION (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Data collected and made available for a specified function
- **STE:** THIS MANUAL CONTAINS INFORMATION ABOUT MAINTENANCE PROCEDURES.

#### INITIAL (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** That is related to the start
- **STE:** MOVE THE LEVER BACK TO ITS INITIAL POSITION.

#### INITIALLY (adv) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** At the start
- **STE:** INITIALLY, THE SPEED INCREASES TO 3000 RPM.

#### INJURY (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Damage to a person
- **STE:** BEND THE ENDS OF THE LOCKING WIRE TO PREVENT INJURY.

#### INNER (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Nearer to the center of an object
- **STE:** THE INNER SURFACE OF THE PART IS BLACK.

#### INPUT (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** The data, power, or energy put into equipment or a system
- **STE:** THE UNIT COMPARES THE INPUTS FROM THE TWO SENSORS TO THE COMPUTER.

#### SPECIFIED (adj) — APPROVED

- > **STE:** IF TOLERANCES ARE NOT SPECIFIED, REFER TO GENERAL TOLERANCE VALUES.
- > **Non-STE:** If tolerances are not indicated, refer to general tolerance values.

#### incomplete (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** NOT FULL
- > **STE:** IF THE ACTUATOR TRAVEL IS NOT FULL, ADJUST THE RODS AGAIN.
- > **Non-STE:** If the actuator travel is incomplete, readjust the rods.
- **Approved alternative:** NOT COMPLETED
- > **STE:** IF THE ADJUSTMENT IS NOT COMPLETED, THE VALUES SHOWN ON THE INDICATOR WILL BE INCORRECT.
- > **Non-STE:** If the adjustment is incomplete, the values shown on the indicator will be incorrect.
- **Approved alternative:** ALL (adj)
- > **STE:** IF YOU DO NOT HAVE ALL THE RESULTS, DO THE TEST AGAIN.
- > **Non-STE:** If the results are incomplete, do the test again.

#### incorporate (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** INCLUDE (v)
- > **STE:** ALL MODELS INCLUDE A MICROPROCESSOR CONTROL UNIT.
- > **Non-STE:** All models incorporate a microprocessor control unit.
- **Approved alternative:** HAVE (v)
- > **STE:** SOME RELAYS HAVE A POTENTIOMETER.
- > **Non-STE:** Some relays incorporate a potentiometer.

#### incorporation (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** INCLUDE (v)

#### increase (n) — UNAPPROVED

#### incur (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** CAUSE (v)

- > **Non-STE:** Removal of the unit before 24 hours can incur damage.

#### independent (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** INDEPENDENTLY (adv)

- > **Non-STE:** The operation of this unit is independent.
- **Approved alternative:** INDEPENDENTLY (adv)

#### indicate (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** SHOW (v)

#### induce (v) — UNAPPROVED
