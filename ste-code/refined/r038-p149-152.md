#### A (art) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Function word — indefinite article.
- **Approved example:** A FUEL PUMP IS INSTALLED IN ZONE 10.

#### abaft (prep) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead: AFT OF (prep)**
  > **STE:** THE CONTROL UNIT IS INSTALLED AFT OF THE FLIGHT COMPARTMENT.
  > **Non-STE:** The control unit is installed abaft the flight compartment.

#### abandon (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead:**
  - **GO (v)**
    > **STE:** IF THERE IS A FIRE, IMMEDIATELY GO TO A SAFE AREA.
    > **Non-STE:** If there is a fire, immediately abandon the area.
  - **STOP (v)**
    > **STE:** IF THE VALUES ARE INCORRECT, STOP THE TEST PROCEDURE.
    > **Non-STE:** If the values are incorrect, abandon the test procedure.

#### abate (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead: DECREASE (v)**
  > **STE:** WHEN THE WIND SPEED DECREASES TO LESS THAN 30 KNOTS, YOU CAN OPEN THE CARGO DOOR.
  > **Non-STE:** When the wind speed abates to less than 30 knots, you can open the cargo door.

#### ability (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead: CAN (v)**
  > **STE:** ONE GENERATOR CAN SUPPLY POWER FOR ALL THE SYSTEMS.
  > **Non-STE:** One generator has the ability to supply power for all the systems.

#### able (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead: CAN (v)**
  > **STE:** IF YOU CAN START THE ENGINE, DO THE APPLICABLE TESTS.
  > **Non-STE:** If you are able to start the engine, do the applicable tests.

#### abnormal (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead:**
  - **UNUSUAL (adj)**
    > **STE:** LISTEN FOR UNUSUAL NOISES.
    > **Non-STE:** Listen for abnormal noises.
  - **INCORRECT (adj)**
    > **STE:** IF YOU FIND AN INCORRECT QUANTITY OF AIR FROM THE VENT MAST, DO A SYSTEM TEST.
    > **Non-STE:** If you find an abnormal quantity of air from the vent mast, do a system test.

#### abnormality (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead: DEFECT (TN)**
  > **STE:** EXAMINE THE SEAL FOR DEFECTS.
  > **Non-STE:** Examine the seal for abnormalities.

#### ABOUT (prep) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Concerned with.
- **Approved example:** FOR DATA ABOUT THE LOCATION OF CIRCUIT BREAKERS, REFER TO THE WIRING LIST.
- **For other meanings, use:**
  - **APPROXIMATELY (adv)**
    > **STE:** DRAIN APPROXIMATELY 2 LITERS OF FUEL FROM THE TANK.
    > **Non-STE:** Drain about 2 liters of fuel from the tank.
  - **AROUND (prep)**
    > **STE:** TURN THE SHAFT AROUND ITS AXIS.
    > **Non-STE:** Rotate the shaft about its axis.

#### ABOVE (prep) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** In (or to) a position farther up than something.
- **Approved example:** LIFT THE CYLINDER ABOVE ITS INSTALLED POSITION.
- **For other meanings, use: MORE THAN**
  > **STE:** THE PRESSURE VALUE MUST BE MORE THAN 800 kPa.
  > **Non-STE:** The pressure value must be above 800 kPa.

#### ABRASIVE (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** That can remove material by friction.
- **Approved example:** POLISH THE SURFACE WITH AN ABRASIVE PAPER.

#### abrupt (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead:**
  - **SUDDEN (adj)**
    > **STE:** THE DAMPER PREVENTS SUDDEN MOVEMENT OF THE CONTROL.
    > **Non-STE:** The damper prevents abrupt movement of the control.
  - **SUDDENLY (adv)**
    > **STE:** IF THE ROTORS STOP SUDDENLY, EXAMINE THE INTAKE FOR UNWANTED MATERIAL.
    > **Non-STE:** If the rotors come to an abrupt stop, examine the intake for unwanted material.

#### absence (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead:**
  - **NONE (pron)**
    > **STE:** IF NONE OF THE BRACKETS ARE DAMAGED, CONTINUE THE PROCEDURE.
    > **Non-STE:** In the absence of damage to the brackets, continue the procedure.
  - **NOT (adv)**
    > **STE:** IF THE BRACKETS ARE NOT DAMAGED, CONTINUE THE PROCEDURE.
    > **Non-STE:** In the absence of damage to the brackets, continue the procedure.
  - **NO (adj)**
    > **STE:** IF THERE IS NO CONTAMINATION IN THE FUEL SAMPLE, CONTINUE THE PROCEDURE.
    > **Non-STE:** In the absence of contamination in the fuel sample, continue the procedure.

#### absent (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead:**
  - **MISSING (adj)**
    > **STE:** IF ONE OR MORE BLADES ARE MISSING, MAKE AN ENTRY IN THE ENGINE LOGBOOK.
    > **Non-STE:** If one or more blades are absent, make an entry in the engine logbook.
  - **NO (adj)**
    > **STE:** IF THERE IS NO CONTAMINATION IN THE FUEL SAMPLE, CONTINUE THE PROCEDURE.
    > **Non-STE:** If contamination is absent from the fuel sample, continue the procedure.

#### absolutely (adv) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead: FULLY (adv)**
  > **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE LATCH IS FULLY ENGAGED.
  > **Non-STE:** Ensure the latch is absolutely engaged.

#### ABSORB (v) — APPROVED

- **Forms:** ABSORBS, ABSORBED, ABSORBED
- **Meanings:**
  1. To take up or into: ABSORB THE FLUID WITH A CLEAN CLOTH.
  2. To decrease the effect of: THE SHOCK MOUNT ABSORBS THE VIBRATION.

#### absorption (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead: ABSORB (v)**
  > **STE:** MEASURE THE TIME THAT IS NECESSARY FOR THE SILICA GEL TO ABSORB THE MOISTURE.
  > **Non-STE:** Measure the rate of absorption of the moisture by the silica gel.

#### abundant (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead: LARGE (adj)**
  > **STE:** CLEAN YOUR SKIN WITH A LARGE QUANTITY OF CLEAN WATER.
  > **Non-STE:** Clean your skin with abundant clean water.

#### abut (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead: TOUCH (v)**
  > **STE:** THE BIN TOUCHES THE FORWARD HINGE SURFACE.
  > **Non-STE:** The bin abuts the forward hinge surface.

#### accelerate (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead:**
  - **INCREASE (v)**
    > **STE:** A HIGHER TEMPERATURE INCREASES THE SPEED OF EVAPORATION.
    > **Non-STE:** Evaporation is accelerated by a higher temperature.
  - **FASTER (adj)**
    > **STE:** TO MAKE THE CURING PROCESS FASTER, APPLY HEAT TO THE COMPOUND.
    > **Non-STE:** The curing process of the compound can be accelerated by heating.

#### ACCEPT (v) — APPROVED

- **Forms:** ACCEPTS, ACCEPTED, ACCEPTED
- **Meaning:** To make a decision that something is satisfactory.
- **Approved example:** ACCEPT THE RELAY IF IT IS SERVICEABLE.

#### acceptable (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead:**
  - **PERMITTED (adj)**
    > **STE:** A VALUE OF 2 mm IS PERMITTED.
    > **Non-STE:** A value of 2 mm is acceptable.
  - **SATISFACTORY (adj)**
    > **STE:** IF THE CONDITION OF THE CAP IS NOT SATISFACTORY, REPLACE IT.
    > **Non-STE:** Replace the cap if its condition is not acceptable.
  - **SERVICEABLE (adj)**
    > **STE:** BEFORE YOU INSTALL THE UNIT, MAKE SURE THAT IT IS SERVICEABLE.
    > **Non-STE:** Before installation, make sure that the unit is acceptable.

#### acceptance (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead: ACCEPT (v)**
  > **STE:** BEFORE YOU ACCEPT THE UNIT, DO THE SPECIFIED TEST PROCEDURE.
  > **Non-STE:** Before acceptance of the unit, do the specified test procedure.

#### ACCESS (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** The ability to go into or near.
- **Approved example:** GET ACCESS TO THE ACCUMULATOR FOR THE No. 1 HYDRAULIC SYSTEM.

#### accessible (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Use instead: ACCESS (n)**
  > **STE:** TURN THE COVER UNTIL YOU CAN GET ACCESS TO THE JACKS THAT HAVE + AND - MARKS.
  > **Non-STE:** Rotate the cover until the jacks marked by + and - are accessible.

#### ACCIDENT (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** An occurrence that causes injury or damage.
- **Approved example:** TO PREVENT ACCIDENTS, MAKE SURE THAT THE PINS ARE INSTALLED.
