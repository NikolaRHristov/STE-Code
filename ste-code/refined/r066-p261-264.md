# ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English

## Dictionary

### Approved Entries

#### GET (v)

- **Inflections:** GETS, GOT (No other verb forms.)
- **Meaning:** To obtain, to come into the state of having
- **Example:**
  > **STE:** IF YOU GET THE FLUID ON YOUR SKIN, FLUSH YOUR SKIN IMMEDIATELY WITH CLEAN WATER.
- **Note:** Do not use this word to mean BECOME, GO, DECREASE, INCREASE. Refer to rule 9.2 and the entries from "get" until "get to" that follow.

#### GIVE (v)

- **Inflections:** GIVES, GAVE, GIVEN
- **Meaning:** To provide
- **Example:**
  > **STE:** THIS SECTION GIVES THE CLEANING PROCEDURES FOR THE DISASSEMBLED PARTS.

#### GLOSSY (adj)

- **Inflections:** GLOSSIER, GLOSSIEST
- **Meaning:** Smooth and shiny
- **Example:**
  > **STE:** APPLY THE PATCH WITH THE MATT SIDE AGAINST THE GLOSSY SURFACE.

#### GO (v)

- **Inflections:** GOES, WENT (No other verb forms.)
- **Meaning:** To move to or from something
- **Example:**
  > **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE POINTER GOES OUT OF VIEW.

#### GO OFF (v)

- **Inflections:** GOES OFF, WENT OFF (No other verb forms.)
- **Meaning:** To become dark when an internal power source is de-energized
- **Example:**
  > **STE:** THE ANNUNCIATOR LIGHT GOES OFF.

#### GOOD (adj)

- **Inflections:** BETTER, BEST
- **Meaning:** That is satisfactory
- **Example:**
  > **STE:** THIS MATERIAL GIVES GOOD PROTECTION FROM CORROSION.

### Unapproved Entries

#### generally (adv)

- **Approved alternative:** USUALLY (adv)
- **Example:**
  > **STE:** YOU USUALLY REPLACE MEMORY MODULES DURING MAINTENANCE.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Memory modules are generally replaced during maintenance.

#### generate (v)

- **Approved alternatives:**
  - BE (v)
  - GIVE (v)
  - SUPPLY (v)
- **Examples:**
  > **STE:** THERE MUST BE NO ERRORS WHEN YOU OPEN THE FILE.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** The file opening must not generate errors.
  >
  > **STE:** IF THE HYDRAULIC PUMP DOES NOT GIVE A PRESSURE OF 3000 psi, USE THE ELECTRIC PUMP.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** If the hydraulic pump fails to generate a pressure of 3000 psi, use the electric pump.
  >
  > **STE:** THE BATTERIES SUPPLY 28 V DC.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** The batteries generate 28 V DC.

#### generous (adj)

- **Approved alternatives:**
  - LARGE (adj)
  - THICK (adj)
- **Examples:**
  > **STE:** A LARGE QUANTITY OF COMPOUND IS NECESSARY.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** A generous amount of compound will be necessary.
  >
  > **STE:** APPLY A THICK LAYER OF GREASE.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Apply a generous amount of grease. If it is possible, be accurate.

#### generously (adv)

- **Approved alternatives:**
  - LARGE (adj)
  - THICK (adj)
- **Examples:**
  > **STE:** APPLY A LARGE QUANTITY OF SEALANT TO THE REPAIR PATCH.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Apply sealant generously to the repair patch.
  >
  > **STE:** APPLY A THICK LAYER OF GREASE.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Apply grease generously. If it is possible, be accurate.

#### gentle (adj)

- **Approved alternatives:**
  - CAREFULLY (adv)
  - LIGHTLY (adv)
- **Examples:**
  > **STE:** TURN THE POTENTIOMETER CAREFULLY.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Rotate the potentiometer with a gentle movement.
  >
  > **STE:** TAP THE SURFACE LIGHTLY.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Give the surface a gentle tap.

#### gently (adv)

- **Approved alternatives:**
  - CAREFULLY (adv)
  - LIGHTLY (adv)
- **Examples:**
  > **STE:** CAREFULLY PULL THE ELECTRICAL WIRE OUT FROM THE RUBBER SEAL.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Gently pull out the electrical wire from the rubber seal.
  >
  > **STE:** LIGHTLY TAP THE TOP SURFACE UNTIL THE PARTS ALIGN.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Gently tap the top surface until the parts align.

#### genuine (adj)

- **Approved alternative:** CORRECT (adj)
- **Example:**
  > **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE INDICATION ON THE GAUGE IS CORRECT.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Make sure that the reading on the gauge is genuine.

#### get (v)

- **Approved alternative:** BECOME (v)
- **Example:**
  > **STE:** DO NOT LET THE HEATER BECOME TOO HOT.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Do not let the heater get too hot.

#### get away (v)

- **Approved alternative:** GO AWAY
- **Example:**
  > **STE:** BEFORE THE ENGINE STARTS, GO AWAY FROM THE EXHAUST AREA.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Get away from the exhaust area before the engine starts.

#### get down (v)

- **Approved alternatives:**
  - DECREASE (v)
  - GO DOWN
- **Examples:**
  > **STE:** IF THE TEMPERATURE DECREASES TO LESS THAN ZERO, DRAIN THE WATER SYSTEM.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** If the temperature gets down to below zero, drain the water system.
  >
  > **STE:** BE CAREFUL WHEN YOU GO DOWN THE LADDER FROM THE COCKPIT TO THE AVIONICS COMPARTMENT.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Be careful when you get down the ladder from the cockpit into the avionics compartment.

#### get into (v)

- **Approved alternative:** GO INTO
- **Example:**
  > **STE:** INSTALL CAPS ON THE RECEPTACLES TO MAKE SURE THAT UNWANTED MATERIAL DOES NOT GO INTO THE RECEPTACLES.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Install caps to make sure that foreign objects do not get into the receptacles.

#### get off (v)

- **Approved alternative:** MOVE OFF
- **Example:**
  > **STE:** BEFORE YOU MOVE THE AILERONS, TELL ALL PERSONS TO MOVE OFF THE WINGS.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Before you move the ailerons, tell all persons to get off the wings.

#### get to (v)

- **Approved alternatives:**
  - BE (v)
  - GO (v)
  - INCREASE (v)
- **Examples:**
  > **STE:** THE MOTOR STOPS WHEN THE LIMIT SWITCHES ARE AT THE END OF THEIR TRAVEL.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** The motor stops when the limit switches get to the end of their travel.
  >
  > **STE:** GO TO THE TOOL ROOM FOR THE NECESSARY TOOLS.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Get to the tool room for the necessary tools.
  >
  > **STE:** WHEN THE TEMPERATURE OF THE VENTILATION AIR INCREASES TO 27 °C, THE DISCHARGE VALVE OPENS.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** When the temperature of the ventilation air gets to 27 °C, the discharge valve opens.

#### gleam (v)

- **Approved alternative:** SHINY (adj)
- **Example:**
  > **STE:** POLISH THE SURFACE WITH A SOFT CLOTH UNTIL IT BECOMES SHINY.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Polish the surface with a soft cloth until it gleams.

#### glitch (n)

- **Approved alternatives:**
  - ERROR (n)
  - FAILURE (TN)
  - UNSERVICEABLE (adj)
- **Examples:**
  > **STE:** IF THERE IS AN ERROR IN THE SYSTEM, THE SCREEN SHOWS: "NO GO."
  >
  > **Non-STE:** If there is a glitch in the system, the screen shows: "NO GO."
  >
  > **STE:** IF THERE IS A FAILURE IN THE SYSTEM, THE SCREEN SHOWS: "NO GO."
  >
  > **Non-STE:** If there is a glitch in the system, the screen shows: NO GO.
  >
  > **STE:** IF THE SYSTEM IS UNSERVICEABLE, THE SCREEN SHOWS: "NO GO."
  >
  > **Non-STE:** If there is a glitch in the system, the screen shows: NO GO.

#### gloss (n)

- **Approved alternative:** SHINY (adj)
- **Example:**
  > **STE:** POLISH THE SURFACE UNTIL IT IS VERY SHINY.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Polish the surface to a high gloss.

#### glow (v)

- **Approved alternative:** BE (v)
- **Examples:**
  > **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE INDICATOR LIGHT IS ON.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Make sure that the indicator light glows.
  >
  > **STE:** ADJUST THE POTENTIOMETER UNTIL THE LIGHT IS DIM.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Adjust the potentiometer until the light glows dimly.

#### glue (v)

- **Approved alternatives:**
  - BOND (v)
  - ATTACH (v)
- **Examples:**
  > **STE:** BOND THE PATCH TO THE SURFACE WITH THE APPLICABLE GLUE.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Glue the patch to the surface.
  >
  > **STE:** ATTACH THE PROTECTIVE PLATE TO THE SURFACE WITH ADHESIVE MATERIAL.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Glue the protective plate to the surface.

#### gouge (v)

- **Approved alternative:** GOUGE (TN)
- **Example:**
  > **STE:** IF A GOUGE OCCURS IN THE BLADE DURING REMOVAL, REPLACE THE BLADE.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** If the blade was gouged during removal, replace it.

#### govern (v)

- **Approved alternative:** CONTROL (v)
- **Example:**
  > **STE:** THIS COMPONENT CONTROLS THE ENGINE SPEED.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** This component governs the engine speed.

#### grab (v)

- **Approved alternatives:**
  - USE (v)
  - HOLD (v)
- **Examples:**
  > **STE:** USE A SCREWDRIVER TO RELEASE THE CLIPS.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Grab a screwdriver to release the clips.
  >
  > **STE:** HOLD THE HANDLE.
  >
  > **Non-STE:** Grab the handle.
