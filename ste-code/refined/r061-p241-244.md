## Dictionary

#### exercise (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** MAKE SURE (v), CAREFUL (adj)

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT YOU DO NOT GET ACID ON YOUR SKIN.
> **Non-STE:** Exercise caution not to allow acid to contact skin.

> **STE:** BE CAREFUL THAT YOU DO NOT GET ACID ON YOUR SKIN.
> **Non-STE:** Exercise caution not to allow acid to contact skin.

#### exert (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** APPLY (v), PUT (v)

> **STE:** APPLY A LIGHT FORCE TO THE PEDALS.
> **Non-STE:** Exert a light force on the pedals.

> **STE:** PUT PRESSURE ON THE WOUND.
> **Non-STE:** Exert pressure on the wound.

#### exhale (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** BREATHE (v)

> **STE:** BREATHE INTO THE OXYGEN MASK.
> **Non-STE:** Exhale into the oxygen mask.

#### EXHAUST (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** The gas from an engine.

> **STE:** THE EXHAUST FROM THE APU IS DANGEROUS.

#### exhaust (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** REMOVE (v), DECREASE (v), RELEASE (v)

> **STE:** USE A FAN TO REMOVE THE GASES.
> **Non-STE:** Exhaust the gases using a fan.

> **STE:** DECREASE THE NITROGEN PRESSURE TO 120 psi.
> **Non-STE:** Exhaust the nitrogen pressure to 120 psi.

> **STE:** RELEASE THE HYDRAULIC PRESSURE IN THE ACCUMULATOR.
> **Non-STE:** Exhaust all the hydraulic pressure in the accumulator.

#### exhaustive (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** ALL (adj), FULL (adj)

> **STE:** ALL THE TEST CONDITIONS ARE GIVEN IN PARAGRAPH 2.0.
> **Non-STE:** An exhaustive list of test conditions is given at paragraph 2.0.

> **STE:** THIS IS NOT A FULL LIST.
> **Non-STE:** The list is not exhaustive.

#### exist (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** BE (v)

> **STE:** THERE ARE TWO DIFFERENT PROCEDURES FOR THIS REPAIR.
> **Non-STE:** Two different procedures exist for this repair.

#### EXIT (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** An opening to go out of a space.

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT ALL THE EMERGENCY EXITS ARE CLEAR OF UNWANTED OBJECTS.

#### EXPAND (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To increase in dimension, volume, or time.
- **Forms:** EXPANDS, EXPANDED, EXPANDED

> **STE:** THE BELLOWS MUST EXPAND.

> **STE:** HEAT EXPANDS THE GAS IN THE CONTAINER.

#### expect (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** POSSIBLE (adj)

> **STE:** IF IT IS POSSIBLE THAT THE TEMPERATURE WILL DECREASE TO -20 DEGREES C, REMOVE THE BATTERIES.
> **Non-STE:** If temperature is expected to drop to -20 degrees C, remove the batteries.

#### expel (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** REMOVE (v), BLEED (v), RELEASE (v)

> **STE:** REMOVE THE AIR BUBBLES FROM THE COMPOUND WITH A SPATULA.
> **Non-STE:** Expel the air bubbles from the compound with a spatula.

> **STE:** BLEED ALL NITROGEN FROM THE SYSTEM.
> **Non-STE:** Expel all nitrogen from the system.

> **STE:** RELEASE ALL NITROGEN.
> **Non-STE:** Expel all nitrogen.

#### EXPIRED (adj) — APPROVED

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE SHELF LIFE IS NOT EXPIRED.

#### explain (v) — UNAPPROVED

> **Non-STE:** The technician will explain the sensor calibration procedure to the operator.

#### EXPLOSION (n) — APPROVED

> **STE:** THIS CONDITION CAN CAUSE AN EXPLOSION.

#### EXPLOSIVE (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** That can cause an explosion.

> **STE:** THE SAFETY PRECAUTIONS THAT FOLLOW ARE APPLICABLE TO EXPLOSIVE ITEMS.

#### EXTINGUISH (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To stop burning.
- **Forms:** EXTINGUISHES, EXTINGUISHED, EXTINGUISHED

> **STE:** TO EXTINGUISH A FIRE IN THE ENGINE, ACTIVATE THE FIRE SUPPRESSION SYSTEM IMMEDIATELY.

> **STE:** WHEN THE FLAME EXTINGUISHES, STOP THE FUEL SUPPLY.

For lights or lamps, use:

> **STE:** THE "AUTO BRK" PUSHBUTTON LIGHT GOES OFF.
> **Non-STE:** The AUTO BRK pushbutton light extinguishes.

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE GREEN LIGHT ON THE PANEL IS OFF.
> **Non-STE:** Make sure that the green light on the panel extinguishes.
