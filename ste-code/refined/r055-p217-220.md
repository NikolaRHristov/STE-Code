# Dictionary (D–DISPOSE)

> **Source:** ASD-STE100 Issue 9, January 2025
> **Pages:** 217–220 of 434

---

## Approved Words

#### DIFFERENT (adj)

- **Meaning:** Not the same
- **STE:** IF THE INDICATIONS ARE DIFFERENT, DO THESE STEPS.

#### DIFFERENTLY (adv)

- **Meaning:** In a different manner
- **STE:** THE TWO EXTRACTORS OPERATE DIFFERENTLY.

#### DIGITAL (adj)

- **Meaning:** Related to digits
- **STE:** CONNECT THE PRODS OF THE DIGITAL VOLTMETER.

#### DIGITALLY (adv)

- **Meaning:** Given by digits
- **STE:** THE VALUE IS SHOWN DIGITALLY ON THE DISPLAY.

#### DIM (adj)

- **Meaning:** Not bright (DIMMER, DIMMEST)
- **STE:** DURING NIGHT OPERATION, MAKE SURE THAT THE PANEL LIGHTS ARE DIM.

#### DIMENSION (n)

- **Meaning:** The result when something is measured in one direction
- **STE:** DIMENSION "A" MUST NOT BE MORE THAN 50 PERCENT OF DIMENSION "D."

#### DIMLY (adv)

- **Meaning:** Not bright
- **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE LIGHT COMES ON DIMLY.

#### DIRECTION (n)

- **Meaning:** The line on which something moves or is pointed
- **STE:** THE ARROW ON THE VALVE BODY SHOWS THE DIRECTION OF THE FLOW.

#### DIRECTLY (adv)

- **Meaning:** In a direct procedure or by direct effect
- **STE:** DO NOT FILL THE SYSTEM DIRECTLY FROM A HIGH-PRESSURE STORAGE CYLINDER.

#### DIRTY (adj)

- **Meaning:** Not clean (DIRTIER, DIRTIEST)
- **STE:** A DIRTY CONTACT IN THE CONNECTION UNITS WILL CAUSE THEM TO OPERATE INCORRECTLY.

#### DISARM (v)

- **Forms:** DISARMS, DISARMED, DISARMED
- **Meaning 1:** To remove armaments
  - **STE:** DISARM THE AIRCRAFT.
- **Meaning 2:** To prevent automatic operation
  - **STE:** DISARM THE LIFT DUMPER.

#### DISASSEMBLE (v)

- **Forms:** DISASSEMBLES, DISASSEMBLED, DISASSEMBLED
- **Meaning:** To take an assembly apart
- **STE:** DISASSEMBLE THE VALVE ASSEMBLY.

#### DISCARD (v)

- **Forms:** DISCARDS, DISCARDED, DISCARDED
- **Meaning:** To not use again
- **STE:** REMOVE THE O-RINGS AND DISCARD THEM.

#### DISCONNECT (v)

- **Forms:** DISCONNECTS, DISCONNECTED, DISCONNECTED
- **Meaning:** To separate something from the thing that it is connected to, or to become separated
- **STE:** DISCONNECT THE POWER SUPPLY.
- **STE:** WHEN YOU TOUCH THIS ICON, THE COMPUTER DISCONNECTS FROM THE INTERNET.

#### DISENGAGE (v)

- **Forms:** DISENGAGES, DISENGAGED, DISENGAGED
- **Meaning:** To release or become released from something that engages
- **STE:** BEFORE YOU START THE ENGINE, DISENGAGE THE CLUTCH.
- **STE:** IF THERE IS A MALFUNCTION, THE GEARS WILL AUTOMATICALLY DISENGAGE.

#### DISPLAY (n)

- **Meaning:** A visual indication
- **STE:** A NUMBER IN THE TOP LEFT CORNER OF THE DISPLAY REFERS TO THE RELATED PAGE.

## Unapproved Words

#### differentiate (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** IDENTIFY (v)

> **STE:** LETTERS IDENTIFY THE WIRE SEGMENTS.
> **Non-STE:** Letters are used to differentiate between the wire segments.

#### difficult (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** NOT EASY

> **STE:** IF IT IS NOT EASY TO INSTALL THE RIGGING PIN, ADJUST THE LENGTH OF THE ROD.
> **Non-STE:** If the rigging pin is difficult to install, adjust the length of the rod.

- **Approved alternative:** NOT EASILY

> **STE:** IF YOU CANNOT INSTALL THE RIGGING PIN EASILY, ADJUST THE LENGTH OF THE ROD.
> **Non-STE:** If the rigging pin is difficult to install, adjust the length of the rod.

#### difficulty (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** NOT EASY

> **STE:** IF IT IS NOT EASY TO REMOVE THE BOLT, APPLY PENETRANT OIL.
> **Non-STE:** If there is difficulty in removing the bolt, apply penetrant oil.

- **Approved alternative:** NOT EASILY

> **STE:** IF YOU CANNOT REMOVE THE BOLT EASILY, APPLY PENETRANT OIL.
> **Non-STE:** If there is difficulty in removing the bolt, apply penetrant oil.

#### dilute (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** MIX (v)

> **STE:** MIX THE PRIMER WITH SOLVENT.
> **Non-STE:** Dilute the primer with solvent.

- **Approved alternative:** ADD (v)

> **STE:** DO NOT ADD MORE THAN AN EQUAL PART OF WATER TO THE ANTIFREEZE.
> **Non-STE:** Do not dilute the antifreeze with more than an equal part of water.

#### dim (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** DECREASE (v)

> **STE:** DECREASE THE LIGHT GRADUALLY.
> **Non-STE:** Dim the light gradually.

#### diminish (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** DECREASE (v)

> **STE:** IF THE HYDRAULIC PRESSURE DECREASES, CLOSE THE VALVE.
> **Non-STE:** If the hydraulic pressure diminishes, close the valve.

#### dip (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** MOMENTARILY (adv)

> **STE:** PUT THE ELEMENT INTO THE SOLVENT MOMENTARILY.
> **Non-STE:** Dip the element in solvent.

#### direct (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** POINT (v)

> **STE:** POINT THE OPEN END OF THE DRAIN HOSE AWAY FROM THE STRUCTURE.
> **Non-STE:** Direct the open end of the drain hose away from the structure.

#### disappear (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** VIEW (n)

> **STE:** THE HORIZONTAL BAR GOES OUT OF VIEW.
> **Non-STE:** The horizontal bar disappears from the window.

#### discharge (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** RELEASE (v)

> **STE:** RELEASE PRESSURE FROM THE TANKS.
> **Non-STE:** Discharge pressure from the tanks.

- **Approved alternative:** GO (v)

> **STE:** THE UNWANTED AIR GOES OVERBOARD.
> **Non-STE:** The unwanted air discharges overboard.

#### discontinue (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** STOP (v)

> **STE:** STOP THE TEST PROCEDURE.
> **Non-STE:** Discontinue the test procedure.

#### discover (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** FIND (v)

> **STE:** IF YOU FIND CORROSION DURING THE INSPECTION, REMOVE IT.
> **Non-STE:** If you discover corrosion during the inspection, remove it.

#### discrepancy (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** DIFFERENCE (n)

> **STE:** IF THERE IS A DIFFERENCE OF MORE THAN 5 mm, MAKE SURE THAT THE ADJUSTMENT IS CORRECT.
> **Non-STE:** If the discrepancy is greater than 5 mm, check the adjustment.

#### disinfect (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** DISINFECTANT (TN)

> **STE:** USE DISINFECTANT TO CLEAN THE MASK ASSEMBLIES.
> **Non-STE:** Disinfect the mask assemblies.

#### dismantle (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** DISASSEMBLE (v)

> **STE:** DISASSEMBLE THE BRAKE UNIT IN THE WORKSHOP.
> **Non-STE:** Dismantle the brake unit in the workshop.

#### dispatch (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** SEND (v)

> **STE:** SEND A REPORT TO THE INSPECTION DEPARTMENT.
> **Non-STE:** Dispatch a report to the Inspection Department.

#### disperse (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** REMOVE (v)

> **STE:** BLOW THROUGH THE TUBES TO REMOVE THE REMAINING FLUID.
> **Non-STE:** Blow through the tubes to disperse the residual fluid.

#### displace (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** MOVE (v)

> **STE:** THE PISTON MOVES THE FLUID.
> **Non-STE:** Fluid is displaced by the piston.

#### display (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** SHOW (v)

> **STE:** THE COMPASS INDICATORS SHOW THE HEADINGS.
> **Non-STE:** The headings are displayed on the compass indicators.

#### dispose of (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** DISCARD (v)

> **STE:** DISCARD THE USED OIL. REFER TO THE APPLICABLE ENVIRONMENTAL REGULATIONS.
> **Non-STE:** Dispose of the used oil according to the applicable environmental regulations.
