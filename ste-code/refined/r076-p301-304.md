## Dictionary

### APPROVED

#### LIVE (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** That includes explosive material
- **STE:** SOME MAINTENANCE TASKS ARE NOT PERMITTED ON AIRCRAFT THAT HAVE LIVE AMMUNITION.
- For electrical systems, use:
  > **STE:** THE SYSTEM IS ENERGIZED.
  > **Non-STE:** The system has live electrical power.

#### LOCAL (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Applicable to a given location or position
- **STE:** SET THE "LAT" KNOB TO THE LOCAL LATITUDE.

#### LOCALLY (adv) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Applicable to a given location or position
- **STE:** YOU CAN ALSO DO THIS TEST WITH A LOCALLY MADE TOOL.

#### LOCATION (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** A place or site that you can find
- **STE:** FIGURES 1 THRU 4 SHOW THE LOCATIONS OF THE WALKWAYS.

#### LOCK (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To attach something, or hold it in position with a locking device
- **Approved forms:** LOCKS, LOCKED, LOCKED
- **STE:** LOCK THE SWITCH.
- **STE:** AND REMOVE THE KEY.
- **STE:** BEFORE YOU CONTINUE, MAKE SURE THAT THE SWITCH LOCKS.

#### LONG (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** That has a specified or large length, or a specified or large duration
- **Approved forms:** LONGER, LONGEST
- **STE:** USE A TUBE WHICH IS 3.3 FEET LONG.
- **STE:** IF THE TUBE IS TOO SHORT, USE A LONGER ONE.
- **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE TEST SIGNAL IS NOT LONGER THAN 3 TONES.

#### LONGITUDINAL (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Related to the longitudinal axis
- **STE:** ADJUST THE LONGITUDINAL TRIM SYSTEM.

#### LONGITUDINALLY (adv) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** In a longitudinal direction
- **STE:** MOVE THE STAND LONGITUDINALLY.

#### LOOK (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To use your eyes to see or find
- **Approved forms:** LOOKS, LOOKED, LOOKED
- **STE:** LOOK THROUGH THE INSPECTION WINDOW.

#### LOOP (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** A circular shape made in a flexible material
- **STE:** MAKE A LOOP OF WIRE AROUND THE UNIT.

#### LOOSE (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning 1:** On, in, but not attached
  - **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT YOU REMOVE ALL TOOLS, EQUIPMENT, AND LOOSE OBJECTS FROM THE AIR INTAKE.
- **Meaning 2:** Not tight
  - **STE:** DO A CHECK FOR LOOSE FASTENERS.
- For other meanings, use:
  > **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THERE IS TENSION IN THE CABLES.
  > **Non-STE:** Make sure that the cables are not loose.

#### LOOSELY (adv) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Not tightly
- **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE PARTS ARE LOOSELY ASSEMBLED.

#### LOOSEN (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To cause to be not tight
- **Approved forms:** LOOSENS, LOOSENED, LOOSENED
- **STE:** LOOSEN THE SCREWS THAT ATTACH THE CLAMP TO THE SUPPORT.

#### LOW (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** That is of small value
- **Approved forms:** LOWER, LOWEST
- **STE:** THE SWITCH SENSES LOW PRESSURE IN THE DISTRIBUTION CIRCUIT.

#### LOWER (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To move something down
- **Approved forms:** LOWERS, LOWERED, LOWERED
- **STE:** LOWER THE ANTENNA TO GET ACCESS TO THE CABLE CONNECTOR.

### UNAPPROVED

#### little (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative 1:** SMALL (adj)
  > **STE:** THE DIAMETER OF THE TUBE IS TOO SMALL.
  > **Non-STE:** The diameter of the tube is too little.
- **Approved alternative 2:** SHORT (adj)
  > **STE:** INSTALL A SHORT LENGTH OF NEW TUBE.
  > **Non-STE:** Install a little length of new tube.
- **Approved alternative 3:** NOT SUFFICIENT
  > **STE:** IF THE PRESSURE IS NOT SUFFICIENT, THE TEST WILL STOP.
  > **Non-STE:** If the pressure is too little, the test will stop.

#### little (a little) — as adjective — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** SMALL QUANTITY
  > **STE:** ADD A SMALL QUANTITY OF DISINFECTANT TO THE SOLUTION.
  > **Non-STE:** Add a little disinfectant to the solution.

#### little (a little) — as adverb — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** SMALL (adj)
  > **STE:** AFTER YOU REMOVE THE NUT, THE RIB CAN MOVE DOWN A SMALL DISTANCE.
  > **Non-STE:** After you remove the nut, the rib can move down a little.

#### load (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative 1:** INSTALL (v)
  > **STE:** INSTALL THE SPRING IN THE CARTRIDGE.
  > **Non-STE:** Load the spring in the cartridge.
- **Approved alternative 2:** LOAD (TN)
  > **STE:** PUT THE LOAD ON THE SPRING BALANCE.
  > **Non-STE:** Load the spring balance.

#### locate (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative 1:** FIND (v)
  > **STE:** USE TALCUM POWDER TO FIND EXTERNAL LEAKS.
  > **Non-STE:** Use talcum powder to locate external leaks.
- **Approved alternative 2:** ENGAGE (v)
  > **STE:** ENGAGE THE GEAR WHEEL ON THE SHAFT.
  > **Non-STE:** Locate the gear wheel on the shaft.
- **Approved alternative 3:** PUT (v)
  > **STE:** PUT THE CLAMP IN ITS CORRECT POSITION.
  > **Non-STE:** Locate the clamp in its correct position.
