# ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English — Dictionary (leak – listen)

## Dictionary

### APPROVED Entries

#### GO (v)

- **Meaning:** To move or travel from one place to another.

> **STE:** IF FUEL SPILLS ON THE GROUND, IMMEDIATELY GO TO A SAFE AREA.
> **Non-STE:** If fuel spills on the ground, immediately leave the contaminated area.

#### LEAKAGE (n)

- **Meaning:** The quantity of material that goes through a leak.

> **STE:** MEASURE THE LEAKAGE FROM THE OUTLET PORT.

#### LEFT (adj)

- **Meaning:** On the west side when you look north.

> **STE:** INSTALL THE CONTROL PANEL IN THE LEFT CONSOLE.

#### LENGTH (n)

- **Meaning:**
  1. The distance from one end to the other end.
  2. The longer or longest of the three dimensions of an object.

> **STE:** MEASURE THE LENGTH OF THE JACK RAM.

> **STE:** THE DIMENSIONS OF THE UNIT ARE:
> - LENGTH 300 mm
> - WIDTH 90 mm
> - THICKNESS 60 mm.

#### LESS (adj)

- **Meaning:** Of smaller dimension, value, quantity, volume, or number. No other forms of this adjective.

> **STE:** THE FLOW RATE MUST NOT BE LESS THAN 4.0 GPM.

#### LET (v)

- **Meaning:** To give opportunity.
- **Forms:** LETS, LET. No other verb forms.

> **STE:** LET THE ADHESIVE BECOME TACKY.

#### LEVEL (adj)

- **Meaning:** Horizontal to a known datum.

> **STE:** PARK THE VEHICLE ON LEVEL GROUND.

#### LEVEL (n)

- **Meaning:** A horizontal line, plane, surface, or condition.

> **STE:** FILL THE TANK TO THE CORRECT LEVEL (REFER TO FIG. 105).

#### LIFE (n)

- **Meaning:** The time during which you can use an item or object.

> **STE:** THE LIFE OF THE CARTRIDGE IN THE FIRE EXTINGUISHER IS 3 YEARS.

#### LIFT (v)

- **Meaning:** To move something up.
- **Forms:** LIFTS, LIFTED, LIFTED.

> **STE:** LIFT THE COVER CAREFULLY.

#### LIGHT (adj)

- **Meaning:** That has a small mass, weight, or force.
- **Forms:** LIGHTER, LIGHTEST.

> **STE:** APPLY A LIGHT FORCE ON THE RUDDER PEDALS.

For other meanings of "light", use:

- **THIN (adj):**

  > **STE:** APPLY A THIN LAYER OF SEALANT ON THE SEALING SURFACES OF THE CONTAINER.
  > **Non-STE:** Apply a light layer of sealant on the sealing surfaces of the container.

- **SMALL (adj):**

  > **STE:** APPLY A SMALL QUANTITY OF LUBRICANT ON THE MOVING PARTS.
  > **Non-STE:** Apply a light quantity of lubricant on the moving parts.

#### LIGHTING (n)

- **Meaning:** That which gives light to.

> **STE:** A DIMMER UNIT CONTROLS THE PANEL LIGHTING.

#### LIGHTLY (adv)

- **Meaning:** In a light manner.

> **STE:** LIGHTLY RUB THE DEFECTIVE AREA WITH WET ABRASIVE PAPER.

#### LIMIT (n)

- **Meaning:** A specified maximum or minimum quantity, number, time, or distance.

> **STE:** IF THE CLEARANCES ARE NOT IN THE LIMITS GIVEN IN FIG. 4, REFER TO REPAIR SCHEME No. 2.

#### LINEAR (adj)

- **Meaning:** In a straight line.

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE RESULTS ARE LINEAR.

#### LINEARLY (adv)

- **Meaning:** In a straight line.

> **STE:** INCREASE THE PRESSURE LINEARLY.

#### LIQUID (adj)

- **Meaning:** That has the properties of a liquid.

> **STE:** DURING SERVICING, LIQUID OXYGEN FLOWS THROUGH THE STABILIZING CONTAINER.

#### LIQUID (n)

- **Meaning:** A material that is not a gas or a solid.

> **STE:** THE CONVERTER CHANGES THE LIQUID INTO A GAS.

#### LISTEN (v)

- **Meaning:** To use your ears to hear or find.
- **Forms:** LISTENS, LISTENED, LISTENED.

> **STE:** LISTEN FOR THE SIGNAL.

#### MINIMUM (n)

- **Meaning:** The smallest possible quantity, number, or time.

> **STE:** DO STEPS 13 THRU 16 FOR A MINIMUM OF THREE TIMES.
> **Non-STE:** Do steps 13 thru 16 at least three times.

### UNAPPROVED Entries

#### leak (v)

- **Approved alternative:** LEAK (n)

> **STE:** IF THERE ARE LEAKS IN THE SYSTEM, REPAIR THEM.
> **Non-STE:** If the system is leaking, repair it.

#### least (adv)

- **Approved alternative:** MINIMUM (adj)

> **STE:** REPAIR THE AREA WITH THE MINIMUM DAMAGE FIRST.
> **Non-STE:** Repair the least damaged area first.

#### least (at least)

- **Approved alternatives:** MINIMUM (adj), MINIMUM (adv)

> **STE:** THE MINIMUM TIME BETWEEN CHECKS IS TWO MINUTES.
> **Non-STE:** The time between checks is at least two minutes.

#### leave (v)

- **Approved alternatives:** KEEP (v), LET …. STAY (v)

> **STE:** KEEP THE VENT VALVES OPEN.
> **Non-STE:** Leave the vent valves open.

> **STE:** LET THE VENT VALVES STAY OPEN.
> **Non-STE:** Leave the vent valves open.

#### left-hand (adj)

- **Approved alternative:** LEFT (adj)

> **STE:** SET THE LEFT SWITCH TO "ON."
> **Non-STE:** Set the left-hand switch to ON.

#### legible (adj)

- **Approved alternative:** READ (v)

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT YOU CAN READ THE IDENTIFICATION PLATES.
> **Non-STE:** Make certain that the identification plates are legible.

#### lengthen (v)

- **Approved alternative:** INCREASE (v)

> **STE:** DO NOT TRY TO INCREASE THE LENGTH OF THIS ROD.
> **Non-STE:** Do not try to lengthen this rod.

#### lethal (adj)

- **Approved alternative:** KILL (v)

> **STE:** THE ELECTRICAL DISCHARGE FROM THESE POWER UNITS CAN KILL YOU.
> **Non-STE:** The electrical discharge from these power units can be lethal.

#### level (v)

- **Approved alternative:** LEVEL (adj)

> **STE:** MAKE THE AIRCRAFT LEVEL.
> **Non-STE:** Level the aircraft.

#### liberal (adj)

- **Approved alternatives:** LARGE (adj), THICK (adj)

> **STE:** CLEAN THE WINDOW WITH LARGE QUANTITIES OF WATER AND SOAP.
> **Non-STE:** Clean the window with liberal quantities of water and soap.

> **STE:** APPLY A THICK LAYER OF THE COMPOUND.
> **Non-STE:** Apply a liberal amount of the compound.

#### liberally (adv)

- **Approved alternatives:** LARGE (adj), THICK (adj)

> **STE:** APPLY A LARGE QUANTITY OF THE SOLUTION.
> **Non-STE:** Apply the solution liberally.

> **STE:** APPLY A THICK LAYER OF THE COMPOUND.
> **Non-STE:** Apply the compound liberally.

#### lie (v)

- **Approved alternative:** BE (v)

> **STE:** THE OTHER PART OF THE CLIP IS ALONG THE BARREL.
> **Non-STE:** The other part of the clip lies along the barrel.

#### light (v)

- **Approved alternative:** COME ON

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE FLUID INDICATOR LIGHT COMES ON.
> **Non-STE:** Ensure that the fluid indicator light lights.

#### limitation (n)

- **Approved alternative:** LIMIT (n)

> **STE:** WHEN YOU LIFT THE AIRCRAFT ON JACKS, KEEP THE CENTER OF GRAVITY BETWEEN THESE LIMITS:
> **Non-STE:** When you lift the aircraft on jacks, observe these center of gravity limitations:

#### limited (adj)

- **Approved alternative:** SMALL (adj)

> **STE:** THERE IS ONLY A SMALL NUMBER OF REPAIRS THAT YOU CAN DO.
> **Non-STE:** There is only a limited number of repairs that you can do.

#### link (v)

- **Approved alternatives:** CONNECT (v), ATTACH (v)

> **STE:** CONNECT THE CABLES.
> **Non-STE:** Link the cables.

> **STE:** ATTACH THE HOIST TO THE POWER UNIT.
> **Non-STE:** Link the hoist to the power unit.

#### list (v)

- **Approved alternatives:** RECORD (v), SHOW (v), LIST (TN)

> **STE:** RECORD THE TEST RESULTS.
> **Non-STE:** List the test results.

> **STE:** TABLE 1 SHOWS ALTERNATIVE ADHESIVES THAT YOU CAN USE.
> **Non-STE:** Table 1 lists alternative adhesives that you can use.

> **STE:** MAKE A LIST OF THE PART NUMBERS IN NUMERICAL SEQUENCE.
> **Non-STE:** List the part numbers in numerical sequence.
