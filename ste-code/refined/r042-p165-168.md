# ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English

## Part 2 — Dictionary

### A (continued)

#### APPLICABLE (adj)

- **Approved meaning:** Correct or satisfactory

> **STE:** REFER TO THE TABLE THAT FOLLOWS FOR THE APPLICABLE TORQUE VALUES.

#### application (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** APPLY (v)

> **STE:** APPLY THE SEALANT WITH A WOODEN SPATULA.
> **Non-STE:** The application of sealant must be carried out with a wooden spatula.

#### APPLY (v)

- **Approved meaning:**
  1. To put or spread something on
  2. To use something (for example, pressure, force, load) to cause an effect
- **Forms:** APPLIES, APPLIED, APPLIED

> **STE:** APPLY THE SEALING COMPOUND.

> **STE:** APPLY A FORCE OF 100 N ON THE END OF THE LEVER.

#### appoint (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** TELL (v)

> **STE:** TELL TWO PERSONS TO BE PREPARED TO OPERATE THE FIRE EXTINGUISHERS.
> **Non-STE:** Appoint two persons to be prepared to operate the fire extinguishers.

#### approach (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** GO NEAR

> **STE:** IF IT IS NECESSARY TO GO NEAR THE ENGINE EXHAUST, USE EAR PROTECTION.
> **Non-STE:** If it is necessary to approach the engine exhaust, use ear protection.

#### appropriate (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** APPLICABLE (adj)

> **STE:** LOCK THE WASHER WITH THE APPLICABLE TABS.
> **Non-STE:** Lock the washer with the appropriate tabs.

#### APPROVAL (n)

- **Approved meaning:** That which permits a person or a thing to do something

> **STE:** BEFORE YOU REPAIR THIS UNIT, GET THE APPROVAL OF THE MANUFACTURER.

#### approve (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** APPROVAL (n)

> **STE:** APPROVAL FROM THE DESIGN AUTHORITY IS MANDATORY FOR ALL MODIFICATIONS.
> **Non-STE:** All modifications must be approved by the Design Authority.

#### APPROVED (adj)

- **Approved meaning:** Permitted by an authority

> **STE:** DO THE PROCEDURE IN AN APPROVED AREA.

#### APPROXIMATE (adj)

- **Approved meaning:** Almost correct or accurate

> **STE:** AN APPROXIMATE VALUE IS SATISFACTORY.

#### APPROXIMATELY (adv)

- **Approved meaning:** Almost correct or accurate

> **STE:** DRAIN APPROXIMATELY 2 LITERS OF FUEL.

#### AREA (n)

- **Approved meaning:** A specified surface or location

> **STE:** DO NOT SMOKE IN THE WORK AREA.

#### arise (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative 1:** OCCUR (v)

> **STE:** SHOCK LOADING OF THE ENGINE CAN OCCUR DURING A HEAVY LANDING.
> **Non-STE:** Shock loading of the engine can arise from a heavy landing.

- **Approved alternative 2:** CAUSE (v)

> **STE:** A HEAVY LANDING CAN CAUSE SHOCK LOADING OF THE ENGINE.
> **Non-STE:** Shock loading of the engine can arise from a heavy landing.

#### ARM (v)

- **Approved meaning:**
  1. To install armaments
  2. To prepare for automatic operation
- **Forms:** ARMS, ARMED, ARMED

> **STE:** ARM THE AIRCRAFT.

> **STE:** SET THE SWITCH TO "CLOSE" TO ARM THE CIRCUIT.

#### AROUND (prep)

- **Approved meaning:** On all sides of

> **STE:** PUT THE STRAP CLAMPS AROUND THE CYLINDER.

For other meanings, use:

- **APPROXIMATELY (adv)**

> **STE:** THE TEST BENCH IS APPROXIMATELY 10 FEET FROM THE DOOR.
> **Non-STE:** The test bench is around 10 feet from the door.

#### arrange (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** PUT (v)

> **STE:** PUT THE COMPONENTS IN THIS SEQUENCE:
> **Non-STE:** Arrange the components in this order.

#### arrangement (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative 1:** CONFIGURATION (TN)

> **STE:** THE CONFIGURATION OF THE INSERTS IS RELATED TO THE DIMENSIONS OF THE CONNECTOR SHELL.
> **Non-STE:** The arrangement of the inserts is related to the dimensions of the connector shell.

- **Approved alternative 2:** PREPARE (v)

> **STE:** PREPARE FOR THE LEAK TEST.
> **Non-STE:** Make the arrangements for the leak test.

#### arrest (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative 1:** STOP (v)

> **STE:** STOP THE FLOW FOR 10 SECONDS.
> **Non-STE:** Arrest the flow for 10 seconds.

- **Approved alternative 2:** HOLD (v)

> **STE:** A COTTER PIN HOLDS THE ATTACHMENT BAR.
> **Non-STE:** A cotter pin arrests the attachment bar.

#### arrive (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** GET (v)

> **STE:** TO GET THE CORRECT VALUE, SET THE SWITCH TO POSITION 2.
> **Non-STE:** To arrive at the correct value, set the switch to position 2.

#### ARROW (n)

- **Approved meaning:** A mark that has a point to show direction

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE ARROW POINTS IN THE DIRECTION OF THE FUEL FLOW.

#### article (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** OBJECT (n)

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THERE ARE NO LOOSE OBJECTS IN THE ENGINE AIR INTAKES.
> **Non-STE:** Ensure that there are no loose articles in the engine air intakes.

#### AS (prep)

- **Approved meaning:** In the manner of, to the same quantity, equally

> **STE:** DO NOT USE THE CYLINDER AS A SUPPORT.

#### as (conj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative 1:** BECAUSE (conj)

> **STE:** THE PUMP DID NOT OPERATE BECAUSE THE SHAFT WAS BROKEN.
> **Non-STE:** The pump did not operate as the shaft was broken.

- **Approved alternative 2:** WHILE (conj)

> **STE:** WHILE YOU DO EACH STEP OF THE VALVE TEST, MAKE SURE THAT THERE IS NO LEAKAGE.
> **Non-STE:** As you do each step of the valve test, make sure that there is no leakage.

You can use:

- AS FOLLOWS

> **STE:** ASSEMBLE THE VALVE AS FOLLOWS:

- AS NECESSARY

> **STE:** SET THE LENGTH OF THE CONTROL ROD AS NECESSARY.

- AS SHOWN

> **STE:** MEASURE THE DEPTH OF THE DENT AS SHOWN IN FIGURE 4.

- **AS … AS (conj):** Construction used to show comparison or relation

> **STE:** MOVE THE LEVER AS FAST AS POSSIBLE.

For "as long as," refer to "long (as long as)."

#### ascertain (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** MAKE SURE (v)

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE LIGHT IS OFF.
> **Non-STE:** Ascertain that the light is off.

#### ask (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative 1:** TELL (v)

> **STE:** TELL THE DRIVER TO SET THE SWITCH TO OFF.
> **Non-STE:** Ask the driver to set the switch to off.

- **Approved alternative 2:** SPEAK (v)

> **STE:** SPEAK TO THE LOCAL AIR TRAFFIC CONTROLLER.
> **Non-STE:** Ask the local air traffic controller.

#### ASSEMBLE (v)

- **Approved meaning:** To attach, connect, or put together the parts of something
- **Forms:** ASSEMBLES, ASSEMBLED, ASSEMBLED

> **STE:** ASSEMBLE THE VALVE AS FOLLOWS:

#### ASSEMBLY (n)

- **Approved meaning:** Items that are connected for a specified function

> **STE:** REMOVE THE WHEEL BRAKE ASSEMBLY FROM THE AXLE.

#### assess (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative 1:** CALCULATE (v)

> **STE:** CALCULATE THE QUANTITY OF FUEL THAT IS NECESSARY.
> **Non-STE:** Assess the quantity of fuel that is necessary.

- **Approved alternative 2:** ESTIMATE (n)

> **STE:** MAKE AN ESTIMATE OF THE DAMAGE.
> **Non-STE:** Assess the damage.

#### assessment (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative 1:** ESTIMATE (n)

> **STE:** MAKE AN ESTIMATE OF THE DAMAGE.
> **Non-STE:** Carry out an assessment of the damage.

- **Approved alternative 2:** CALCULATE (v)

> **STE:** CALCULATE THE NUMBER OF SHIMS THAT ARE NECESSARY.
> **Non-STE:** Do an assessment of the number of shims that are necessary.

#### assign (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** GIVE (v)

> **STE:** GIVE A CODE TO EACH CONNECTOR.
> **Non-STE:** Assign a code to each connector.

#### assist (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** HELP (v)

> **STE:** KEEP THE DATA TO HELP YOU WITH SUBSEQUENT PROCEDURES.
> **Non-STE:** Keep the data to assist subsequent procedures.

#### assistance (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative 1:** AID (n)

> **STE:** FOR THIS STEP, MAKE SURE THAT YOU HAVE THE AID OF ONE MORE PERSON.
> **Non-STE:** For this step, you must have the assistance of one more person.

- **Approved alternative 2:** HELP (v)

> **STE:** FOR THIS STEP, MAKE SURE THAT ONE PERSON HELPS YOU.
> **Non-STE:** For this step, you must have the assistance of one person.
