## Approved Words

#### OUTBOARD (adj)

- **Approved meaning:** Farther from the longitudinal axis of something

> **STE:** MEASURE THE MOVEMENT OF THE INBOARD AND OUTBOARD FLAPS.

#### OUTBOARD (adv)

- **Approved meaning:** In, or to, a position farther from the longitudinal axis of something

> **STE:** MOVE THE JACK OUTBOARD TO DISENGAGE IT FROM THE JACKING POINT.

#### OUTBOARD OF (prep)

- **Approved meaning:** At a position farther from the longitudinal axis

> **STE:** PUT THE ACCESS PLATFORM IN A POSITION THAT IS OUTBOARD OF THE FLAP TRACK.

#### OUTDOORS (adv)

- **Approved meaning:** Not in a building

> **STE:** DO THIS WORK OUTDOORS.

#### OUTER (adj)

- **Approved meaning:** Located on the outside or farther from the center of an object

> **STE:** APPLY SEALANT ON THE OUTER SURFACE.

#### OUTPUT (n)

- **Approved meaning:** The data, power, energy that comes out of equipment or a system

> **STE:** THE IMPEDANCE MATCHING TRANSFORMERS SUPPLY 60 W OUTPUT TO THE LOUDSPEAKERS.

#### OVERBOARD (adv)

- **Approved meaning:** Out of a vessel

> **STE:** RELEASE THE AIR OVERBOARD.

#### OVERLAP (n)

- **Approved meaning:** The area in which a part of one surface is on a part of a second surface

> **STE:** IF THE OVERLAP IS MORE THAN 0,01 mm, REPLACE THE PART.

#### OVERRIDE (v)

- **Approved meaning:** To prevent the automatic operation of a part or system
- **Forms:** OVERRIDES, OVERRODE, OVERRIDDEN

> **STE:** MANUALLY OVERRIDE THE START SEQUENCE.

## Unapproved Words

#### outline (v)

- **Alternative:** GIVE (v)

> **STE:** THIS SECTION GIVES YOU THE PROCEDURES THAT ARE NECESSARY TO FIND THE FAULTS.
> **Non-STE:** This section outlines the procedures that are necessary to find the faults.

#### outside (adj)

- **Alternative 1:** EXTERNAL (adj)

> **STE:** THE EXTERNAL TEMPERATURE IS SHOWN ON THE CENTRAL DISPLAY UNIT.
> **Non-STE:** The outside temperature is indicated on the central display unit.

- **Alternative 2:** OUTER (adj)

> **STE:** CLEAN THE OUTER SURFACE WITH WARM WATER.
> **Non-STE:** Clean the outside surface with warm water.

#### outside (adv)

- **Alternative:** OUTDOORS (adv)

> **STE:** DO THIS PROCEDURE OUTDOORS.
> **Non-STE:** Do this procedure outside.

#### outside (n)

- **Alternative 1:** EXTERNAL (adj)

> **STE:** FOLD THE EDGING AGAINST THE EXTERNAL SIDE OF THE INSULATION BLANKET.
> **Non-STE:** Fold the edging against the outside of the insulation blankets.

- **Alternative 2:** OUTER (adj)

> **STE:** FOLD THE EDGING AGAINST THE OUTER SIDE OF THE INSULATION BLANKET.
> **Non-STE:** Fold the edging against the outside of the insulation blankets.

#### outside (prep)

- **Alternative:** NEAR (prep)

> **STE:** WHEN YOU DO WORK IN A FUEL TANK, A SECOND PERSON MUST BE NEAR THE TANK AT ALL TIMES.
> **Non-STE:** When you do work in a fuel tank, a second person must be outside the tank at all times.

#### outwards (adv)

- **Alternative:** OUT (adv)

> **STE:** MOVE THE DOOR OUT AND FORWARD.
> **Non-STE:** Move the door outwards and forwards.

#### over (prep)

- **Alternative 1:** ABOVE (prep)

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE HYDRAULIC FLUID LEVEL IS NOT ABOVE THE "FULL" MARK.
> **Non-STE:** Make sure the hydraulic fluid level is not over the FULL mark.

- **Alternative 2:** ALONG (prep)

> **STE:** APPLY THE WEIGHT EQUALLY ALONG THE STABILIZER SPAN.
> **Non-STE:** The weight must be evenly spread over the stabilizer span.

- **Alternative 3:** ON (prep)

> **STE:** INSTALL THE STOP SLEEVE ON THE SLIDING MEMBER.
> **Non-STE:** Install the stop sleeve over the sliding member.

- **Alternative 4:** MORE THAN

> **STE:** DO NOT INSTALL SHIMS WITH A THICKNESS OF MORE THAN 0.1 mm.
> **Non-STE:** Do not install shims over 0.1 mm thick.

For movement above land or water, use a technical noun:

> **STE:** BEFORE OVER-WATER OPERATION AT LOW ALTITUDES, APPLY CORROSION-PREVENTIVE FLUID TO THE DOOR HINGES OF THE LANDING GEAR.

#### overfill (v)

- **Alternative:** TOO MUCH

> **STE:** DO NOT PUT TOO MUCH FLUID IN THE LUBRICATION HOLE.
> **Non-STE:** Do not overfill the lubrication hole.

#### overfull (adj)

- **Alternative:** TOO FULL

> **STE:** IF THE TANK IS TOO FULL, YOU CANNOT PRESSURIZE IT CORRECTLY.
> **Non-STE:** If the tank is overfull, you cannot pressurize it correctly.

#### overhaul (v)

- **Alternative:** OVERHAUL (TN)

> **STE:** DO AN OVERHAUL OF THE No. 2 ENGINE.
> **Non-STE:** Overhaul the No. 2 engine.

#### overheat (v)

- **Alternative:** TOO HOT

> **STE:** IF YOU PRESSURIZE THE OXYGEN BOTTLE QUICKLY, IT WILL BECOME TOO HOT.
> **Non-STE:** Rapid charging overheats the oxygen bottle.

#### overlap (v)

- **Alternative:** OVERLAP (n)

> **STE:** MAKE AN OVERLAP OF 10 mm.
> **Non-STE:** Overlap the surfaces by 10 mm.

#### overtighten (v)

- **Alternative 1:** TIGHTEN TOO MUCH

> **STE:** DO NOT TIGHTEN THE FITTINGS TOO MUCH.
> **Non-STE:** Do not overtighten the fittings.

- **Alternative 2:** TOO TIGHT

> **STE:** DO NOT MAKE THE FITTINGS TOO TIGHT.
> **Non-STE:** Do not overtighten the fittings.

- **Alternative 3:** TOO TIGHTLY

> **STE:** DO NOT INSTALL THE FITTINGS TOO TIGHTLY.
> **Non-STE:** Do not overtighten the fittings.
