## APPROVED

#### OUTBOARD (adj) — APPROVED

- Meaning: Farther from the longitudinal axis of something
> **STE:** MEASURE THE MOVEMENT OF THE INBOARD AND OUTBOARD FLAPS.

#### OUTBOARD (adv) — APPROVED

- Meaning: In, or to, a position farther from the longitudinal axis of something
> **STE:** MOVE THE JACK OUTBOARD TO DISENGAGE IT FROM THE JACKING POINT.

#### OUTBOARD OF (prep) — APPROVED

- Meaning: At a position farther from the longitudinal axis
> **STE:** PUT THE ACCESS PLATFORM IN A POSITION THAT IS OUTBOARD OF THE FLAP TRACK.

#### OUTDOORS (adv) — APPROVED

- Meaning: Not in a building
> **STE:** DO THIS WORK OUTDOORS.

#### OUTER (adj) — APPROVED

- Meaning: Located on the outside or farther from the center of an object
> **STE:** APPLY SEALANT ON THE OUTER SURFACE.

#### OUTPUT (n) — APPROVED

- Meaning: The data, power, energy that comes out of equipment or a system
> **STE:** THE IMPEDANCE MATCHING TRANSFORMERS SUPPLY 60 W OUTPUT TO THE LOUDSPEAKERS.

#### OVERBOARD (adv) — APPROVED

- Meaning: Out of a vessel
> **STE:** RELEASE THE AIR OVERBOARD.

#### OVERLAP (n) — APPROVED

- Meaning: The area in which a part of one surface is on a part of a second surface
> **STE:** IF THE OVERLAP IS MORE THAN 0,01 mm, REPLACE THE PART.

#### OVERRIDE (v) — APPROVED

- Meaning: To prevent the automatic operation of a part or system
- Forms: OVERRIDES, OVERRODE, OVERRIDDEN
> **STE:** MANUALLY OVERRIDE THE START SEQUENCE.
