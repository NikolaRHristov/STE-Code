## APPROVED Entries

#### SEPARATION (n)

- **Meaning:** The action or result of separating
- **Example:** SEPARATION OF THESE PARTS IS NOT EASY.

#### SEQUENCE (n)

- **Meaning:** The relation of items that follow one after the other in a list or the relation of steps or events that occur one after the other in time
- **Example:** TIGHTEN THE BOLTS IN THE SEQUENCE THAT IS GIVEN IN FIGURE 3.

#### SERRATION (n)

- **Meaning:** One or more notches or teeth as in a saw
- **Example:** MAKE MARKS THAT SHOW THE LOCATION OF THE SERRATIONS.

#### SERVICING (n)

- **Meaning:** The steps necessary to prepare something for operation
- **Example:** BEFORE YOU DO SERVICING OF THE HYDRAULIC SYSTEM, ISOLATE IT.

#### SET (n)

- **Meaning:** A group of related items that you use for the same purpose
- **Example:** USE THE SET OF TOOLS THAT IS SUPPLIED WITH THE UNIT.

#### SET (v)

- **Meaning:** To put something into a given adjustment, condition, or mode
- **Forms:** SETS, SET, SET
- **Example:** SET THE ALTIMETER SCALE TO 1013 mbar.

#### SHAKE (v)

- **Meaning:** To move or cause to move quickly up and down or from side to side
- **Forms:** SHAKES, SHOOK, SHAKEN
- **Examples:**
  - SHAKE THE CONTAINER.
  - THE CONTROL COLUMN STARTS TO SHAKE BEFORE A STALL OCCURS.

#### SHAPE (n)

- **Meaning:** The contour of an object
- **Example:** THE PATCH MUST BE THE SAME SHAPE AS THE CUTOUT.

#### SHARP (adj)

- **Meaning:** That can cut or make a hole
- **Forms:** SHARPER, SHARPEST
- **Example:** MAKE A HOLE WITH A SHARP TOOL.

#### SHEET (n)

- **Meaning:** A piece of material that is thin in relation to its length and width
- **Example:** IF THE SHEETS HAVE DENTS WITH A DEPTH OF MORE THAN 0.5 mm, REMOVE THESE DENTS.

#### SHINY (adj)

- **Meaning:** That can cause a reflection of light
- **Forms:** SHINIER, SHINIEST
- **Example:** CLEAN THE SHINY AREA OF THE OLEO STRUT.

#### SHOCK (n)

- **Meaning:** The sudden effect of energy on a person or object
- **Example:** SHOCKS CAN CAUSE DAMAGE TO THE GYROSCOPE.

#### SHORT (adj)

- **Meaning:** That has small length or duration
- **Forms:** SHORTER, SHORTEST
- **Example:** ATTACH THE SHORT ARM OF THE BELLCRANK TO THE ROD.
- **Example:** AFTER YOU STOP THE POWER SUPPLY, THE TEMPERATURE DECREASES IN A SHORT TIME. THIS TIME MUST NOT BE MORE THAN 30 SECONDS.
- **Usage note:** If it is possible, be accurate.
