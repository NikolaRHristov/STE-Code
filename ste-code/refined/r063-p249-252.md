# ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English — Dictionary (F)

> **Source:** ASD-STE100 Issue 9, January 2025
> **Pages:** 249–252 of 434

## Approved Words

#### FEATHER (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To put a propeller to a position of minimum drag
- **Forms:** FEATHERS, FEATHERED, FEATHERED

> **STE:** PUT THE CONDITION LEVER IN THE FTR POSITION TO FEATHER THE PROPELLER.

#### FEEL (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To touch to find
- **Forms:** FEELS, FELT, FELT

> **STE:** CAREFULLY LIFT THE MAGNETIC LEVEL INDICATOR UNTIL YOU FEEL THE MAGNETS.

#### FILL (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To put into a container or a space to the maximum level or, if specified, to a given level, pressure, or quantity
- **Forms:** FILLS, FILLED, FILLED

> **STE:** FILL THE TANK WITH METHANOL.

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE RESERVOIR FILLS WITH WATER.

#### FILTERED (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** That has gone through a filter

> **STE:** DRY THE UNIT WITH LOW-PRESSURE FILTERED AIR.

#### FIND (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To discover, to examine something so that you know
- **Forms:** FINDS, FOUND, FOUND

> **STE:** YOU CAN FIND THE NUMBERS ON THE SWITCH.

#### FINISH (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** The result of a finishing procedure

> **STE:** DO NOT CAUSE DAMAGE TO THE SURFACE FINISH OF THE HOUSING.

#### FIRE (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** The light and/or heat from a material when it burns

> **STE:** IF THERE IS A FIRE IN THE ENGINE COMPARTMENT, YOU WILL HEAR THE GONG.

#### FIRE (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To ignite, or to operate items that contain an explosive material
- **Forms:** FIRES, FIRED, FIRED

> **STE:** FIRE THE CARTRIDGES.

> **STE:** WHEN YOU PUSH THE AGENT PUSHBUTTON, THE FIRE EXTINGUISHER CARTRIDGE FIRES.

#### FIRST (adv) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Before other persons or things

> **STE:** FILL THE INNER TANKS FIRST.

#### FIT (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** The relation between two related parts, a limit of tolerance

> **STE:** YOU WILL FIND DATA ABOUT FITS AND CLEARANCES IN SECTION 9001.

#### FLAME (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Burning gas

> **STE:** IF YOU SEE FLAMES FROM THE EXHAUST PIPE, STOP THE ENGINE.

#### FLAMMABLE (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** That burns easily

> **STE:** THE SOLVENT IS FLAMMABLE.

#### FLANGE (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** An end surface at an angle

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE FLANGE IS NOT DAMAGED.

#### FLASH (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To come on and go off frequently
- **Forms:** FLASHES, FLASHED, FLASHED

> **STE:** THE RED LIGHTS FLASH IN HALF-SECOND INTERVALS.

#### FLAT (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** That has a continuous surface in the same plane
- **Forms:** FLATTER, FLATTEST

> **STE:** LUBRICATE ONE SIDE OF THE FLAT WASHERS.

#### FLEXIBLE (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** That can easily bend again and again

> **STE:** CONNECT THE FLEXIBLE HOSES TO THE PUMP.

#### FLIGHT (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** The time an aircraft is in the air

> **STE:** AFTER EACH FLIGHT, READ THE COUNTER UNIT INDICATIONS.

#### SUPPLY (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To provide

> **STE:** SUPPLY COOL AIR TO THE CABIN.
> **Non-STE:** Feed cool air to the cabin.

## Unapproved Words

#### feature (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** HAVE (v)

> **STE:** THESE NUTS HAVE A FLAT FLANGE.
> **Non-STE:** These nuts feature a flat flange.

#### feed (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** PUT (v)

> **STE:** PUT THE WIRE THROUGH THE CONDUIT.
> **Non-STE:** Feed the wire through the conduit.

#### few (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** SMALL NUMBER

> **STE:** THIS PROCEDURE IS ONLY FOR REPAIRS THAT INCLUDE A VERY SMALL NUMBER OF RIVETS.
> **Non-STE:** This procedure is only for repairs that include very few rivets.

- **Note:** If it is possible, give an accurate number.

#### few (a few) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** SOME (adj)

> **STE:** LET THE ENGINE OPERATE FOR SOME MINUTES.
> **Non-STE:** Let the engine run for a few minutes.

- **Approved alternative:** AFTER APPROXIMATELY

> **STE:** AFTER APPROXIMATELY 5 SECONDS, THE LIGHT COMES ON AGAIN.
> **Non-STE:** After a few seconds, the light comes on again.

- **Note:** If this alternative is not sufficient, give the range, number, or quantity.

#### file (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** REMOVE (WITH A FILE) (TN)

> **STE:** DO NOT REMOVE TOO MUCH MATERIAL WITH THE FILE.
> **Non-STE:** Take care not to file too deeply.

#### filing (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** PARTICLE (n)

> **STE:** REMOVE ALL THE METAL PARTICLES.
> **Non-STE:** Remove all the metal filings.

#### film (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** LAYER (n)

> **STE:** APPLY A LAYER OF COMPOUND ON THE SURFACE OF THE DISC.
> **Non-STE:** Spread a film of compound on the surface of the disc.

#### filter (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** FILTER (TN)

> **STE:** PUT THE OIL THROUGH A FILTER.
> **Non-STE:** Filter the oil.

#### final (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** LAST (adj)

> **STE:** THE LAST STEP WILL OPEN THE VALVE.
> **Non-STE:** The final action will open the valve.

#### finding (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** RESULT (n)

> **STE:** TELL THE QUALITY DEPARTMENT ABOUT THE RESULTS OF THE ANALYSIS.
> **Non-STE:** Report the findings of the analysis to the quality department.

#### fine (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** SMALL (adj)

> **STE:** ONLY PARTICLES SMALLER THAN 5 µm CAN GO THROUGH THE FILTER.
> **Non-STE:** Only particles finer than 5 microns can go through the filter.

#### finger-tighten (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** TIGHTEN … WITH YOUR FINGERS

> **STE:** TIGHTEN THE NUT WITH YOUR FINGERS.
> **Non-STE:** Finger-tighten the nut.

#### finish (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** COMPLETE (v)

> **STE:** COMPLETE THE TEST.
> **Non-STE:** Finish the test.

#### firmly (adv) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** TIGHTLY (adv)

> **STE:** HOLD THE CYLINDER TIGHTLY.
> **Non-STE:** Hold the cylinder firmly.

#### fit (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** INSTALL (v)

> **STE:** INSTALL THE DUCT.
> **Non-STE:** Fit the duct.

- **Approved alternative:** ATTACH (v)

> **STE:** ATTACH THE PANEL TO THE STRUCTURE WITH THE BOLTS.
> **Non-STE:** Fit the panel to the structure with the bolts.

- **Approved alternative:** CORRECT (adj)

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE REPAIR PLUG HAS THE CORRECT DIMENSIONS.
> **Non-STE:** Ensure the repair plug fits.

#### fix (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** ATTACH (v)

> **STE:** ATTACH THE TRIM WITH DOUBLE-SIDED ADHESIVE TAPE.
> **Non-STE:** Fix the trim with double-sided adhesive tape.

- **Approved alternative:** SET (v)

> **STE:** SET THE LENGTH OF THE CONTROL ROD AS NECESSARY.
> **Non-STE:** Fix the length of the control rod as required.

- **Approved alternative:** REPAIR (v)

> **STE:** REPAIR ALL LOOSE OR DAMAGED UPHOLSTERY.
> **Non-STE:** Fix any loose or torn upholstery.

- **Approved alternative:** INSTALL (v)

> **STE:** INSTALL THE RETAINER BOLTS ON THE RETAINER.
> **Non-STE:** Fix the retainer bolts to the retainer.

#### flatness (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** FLAT (adj)

> **STE:** THE SURFACE MUST BE FLAT TO GET A CORRECT BOND.
> **Non-STE:** A correct bond depends on the surface flatness.

#### flatten (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** FLAT (adj)

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT YOU DO NOT CAUSE THE TUBES TO BECOME FLAT.
> **Non-STE:** Make sure that you do not flatten the tubes.

#### flaw (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** DAMAGE (n)

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THERE IS NO DAMAGE TO THE SURFACE FINISH.
> **Non-STE:** Make sure that there are no flaws in the surface finish.

- **Approved alternative:** DEFECTIVE (adj)

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE SURFACE FINISH IS NOT DEFECTIVE.
> **Non-STE:** Make sure that there are no flaws in the surface finish.

- **Note:** If it is possible, be accurate.

#### flex (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** BEND (v)

> **STE:** THE WING CAN BEND UP AND DOWN DURING FLIGHT.
> **Non-STE:** The wing can flex up and down during flight.

#### flood (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** USE TOO MUCH

> **STE:** DO NOT USE TOO MUCH SOLVENT ON THE AREA.
> **Non-STE:** Do not flood the area with solvent.
