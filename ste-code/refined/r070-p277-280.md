# ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English — Dictionary (impact – incompatible)

> **Source:** ASD-STE100 Issue 9, January 2025
> **Pages:** 277–280 of 434

## Dictionary

#### impact (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** HIT (v)

> **STE:** DO NOT LET THE VIBRATOR TOOL HIT THE SURFACE OF THE SEAL.
> **Non-STE:** Do not allow the vibrator tool to impact the surface of the seal.

- **Approved alternative:** EFFECT (n)

> **STE:** THESE REPAIRS HAVE AN EFFECT ON THE PERFORMANCE OF THE ENGINE.
> **Non-STE:** These repairs impact performance of the engine.

#### impair (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** DECREASE (v)

> **STE:** THE LIFE OF THE UNIT WILL DECREASE IF YOU LET THE UNIT BECOME DIRTY.
> **Non-STE:** The life of the unit is impaired if the unit is allowed to get dirty.

- **Approved alternative:** OPERATE INCORRECTLY

> **STE:** DAMAGE WILL CAUSE THE INSTRUMENT TO OPERATE INCORRECTLY.
> **Non-STE:** Damage will impair operation of the instrument.

- **Approved alternative:** CAUSE DAMAGE

> **STE:** CONTAMINATION OF THE GREASE CAN CAUSE DAMAGE TO THE BEARING.
> **Non-STE:** Contaminants in the grease may impair the bearing.

#### implement (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** DO (v)

> **STE:** IF THE TEST DOES NOT GIVE SATISFACTORY RESULTS, DO THE FAULT ISOLATION PROCEDURE.
> **Non-STE:** If the test does not give satisfactory results, implement the fault isolation procedure.

#### implementation (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** DO (v)

> **STE:** IT IS MANDATORY TO DO THE PROCEDURE IN THIS SERVICE BULLETIN.
> **Non-STE:** The implementation of the procedure in this Service Bulletin is mandatory.

#### IMPORTANT (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** That has a significant effect.

> **STE:** THESE PROCEDURES ARE VERY IMPORTANT.

#### impossible (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** NOT POSSIBLE

> **STE:** THUS, IT IS NOT POSSIBLE TO GIVE A STANDARD VALUE.
> **Non-STE:** Thus, it is impossible to give a standard value.

- **Approved alternative:** CANNOT (v)

> **STE:** YOU CANNOT FILL THE CONTAINERS WHILE THEY ARE INSTALLED ON AIRCRAFT.
> **Non-STE:** It is impossible to recharge containers while installed on aircraft.

#### impregnate (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** SOAK (v)

> **STE:** SOAK THE FILTER ELEMENTS IN OIL.
> **Non-STE:** The filter elements must be impregnated with oil.

#### impression (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** THINK (v)

> **STE:** IF YOU THINK THAT A TIRE HAS LOW PRESSURE, DO THE STEPS THAT FOLLOW:
> **Non-STE:** If you have the impression that a tire has low pressure, do the steps that follow:

#### improper (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** INCORRECT (adj)

> **STE:** INCORRECT ADJUSTMENT OF THE STOP BOLTS ON THE RUDDER PEDALS CAN CAUSE DAMAGE.
> **Non-STE:** Improper adjustment of the stop bolts on the rudder pedals can cause damage.

#### improperly (adv) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** INCORRECTLY (adv)

> **STE:** IF THE HINGE IS INCORRECTLY ADJUSTED, YOU CANNOT CLOSE THE PANEL.
> **Non-STE:** If the hinge is improperly adjusted, the panel will not close.

#### improve (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** BETTER (adj)

> **STE:** DO NOT ADJUST THE PARTS AGAIN TO MAKE THEIR POSITION BETTER.
> **Non-STE:** Do not readjust the parts again to improve their position.

#### impurity (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** UNWANTED MATERIAL

> **STE:** USE A FILTER TO REMOVE UNWANTED MATERIAL FROM THE HYDRAULIC OIL.
> **Non-STE:** Filter the hydraulic oil to remove impurities.

- **Approved alternative:** CONTAMINATION (n)

> **STE:** CONTAMINATION CAN CAUSE A FAILURE.
> **Non-STE:** Impurities may cause a failure.

#### IN (adv) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To, or into a location or position.

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE SWITCHES ARE PUSHED IN.

#### IN (prep) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Function word that shows location, position, condition, time, or limits.

> **STE:** LET THE SWITCH STAY IN THE SET POSITION.
> **STE:** THE COMPENSATORS ARE IN THE WING BAY.
> **STE:** DO NOT PARK THE VEHICLE IN STRONG WINDS.
> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE LIGHT COMES ON IN 5 SECONDS.
> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE CRACKS ARE IN THE LIMITS.

##### IN FRONT OF (prep)

- **Meaning:** In a position ahead of.

> **STE:** PUT CHOCKS IN FRONT OF THE WHEELS.

##### IN PROGRESS (adv)

- **Meaning:** Occurring.

> **STE:** IF AN INTERACTIVE SESSION IS IN PROGRESS, LET IT CONTINUE UNTIL IT IS FULLY COMPLETED.

#### inactive (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** NOT ACTIVE

> **STE:** THE AUTOTHRUST SYSTEM IS ENGAGED BUT IT IS NOT ACTIVE.
> **Non-STE:** The autothrust system is engaged but inactive.

#### inadvertent (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** ACCIDENTAL (adj)

> **STE:** ACCIDENTAL OPERATION OF THE SYSTEM CAN KILL YOU.
> **Non-STE:** Inadvertent operation of the system could cause fatal injuries.

#### inadvertently (adv) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** ACCIDENTALLY (adv)

> **STE:** IF YOU ACCIDENTALLY PUT TOO MUCH FUEL IN THE TANK, OPEN THE DRAIN VALVE.
> **Non-STE:** If the tank is inadvertently overfilled, open the drain valve.

#### INBOARD (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Nearer to the longitudinal axis.

> **STE:** REMOVE THE INBOARD FAIRING OF THE FLAP HINGE.

#### INBOARD (adv) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** In or to a position nearer to the longitudinal axis.

> **STE:** THE LANDING GEAR RETRACTS INBOARD INTO THE BAY.

##### INBOARD OF (prep)

- **Meaning:** At a position nearer to the longitudinal axis.

> **STE:** PUT THE ACCESS PLATFORM IN POSITION BELOW THE ACCESS PANEL THAT IS INBOARD OF THE FLAP TRACK.

#### INCIDENT (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** An important occurrence that can cause damage or have dangerous results.

> **STE:** RECORD ALL INCIDENTS OF WATER FOUND IN THE FUEL.

#### incline (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** SLOPE (n)

> **STE:** YOU CAN ADJUST THE SLOPE OF THE RAMP.
> **Non-STE:** You can adjust the incline of the ramp.

#### INCLUDE (v) — APPROVED

- **Forms:** INCLUDES, INCLUDED
- **Meaning:** To make, or to be, part of.

> **STE:** THIS CHAPTER INCLUDES THE PROCEDURES FOR THE REMOVAL OF THE COMPONENTS.

#### including (prep) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** THRU (prep)

> **STE:** DO TESTS 4 THRU 8 AGAIN.
> **Non-STE:** Repeat from test 4 up to and including test 8.

- **Approved alternative:** WITH (prep)

> **STE:** SEND THE DEFECTIVE COVER, WITH THE OIL SAMPLES, TO THE REPAIR CENTER.
> **Non-STE:** Return the defective cover, including the oil samples, to the repair center.

#### incompatible (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** NOT COMPATIBLE

> **STE:** THIS SOFTWARE IS NOT COMPATIBLE WITH OTHER OPERATING SYSTEMS.
> **Non-STE:** This software is incompatible with other operating systems.

> **STE:** THIS COOLANT IS NOT COMPATIBLE WITH ALUMINUM ALLOYS.
> **Non-STE:** This coolant is incompatible with aluminum alloys.
