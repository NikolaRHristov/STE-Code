## Approved Words

#### CONTAIN (v) — APPROVED

- **Approved meaning:** To have in something or hold in something
- **Forms:** CONTAINS, CONTAINED, CONTAINED

> **STE:** EACH SURVIVAL KIT CONTAINS THESE ITEMS:

#### CONTAINER (n) — APPROVED

- **Approved meaning:** Something that holds fluids, materials, or objects

> **STE:** PUT THE CONTAINER BELOW THE DRAIN PLUG.

#### CONTAMINATION (n) — APPROVED

- **Approved meaning 1:**

> **STE:** REMOVE ALL CONTAMINATION FROM THE HYDRAULIC FLUID WITH A CLASS 5 FILTER.
> **Non-STE:** Filter the contaminated hydraulic fluid through a Class 5 filter.

- **Approved meaning 2:** Unwanted change of the original condition of a material because of chemical or physical agents or a material that causes such a change

> **STE:** DUST CAN CAUSE CONTAMINATION ON ALL FILTERS.

#### CONTENTS (n) — APPROVED

- **Approved meaning:** Something that is in a container

> **STE:** EXAMINE THE CONTENTS OF THE BOX FOR MISSING ITEMS.

#### CONTINUE (v) — APPROVED

- **Approved meaning:** To stay or keep in current condition or operation
- **Forms:** CONTINUES, CONTINUED, CONTINUED

> **STE:** CONTINUE THE TEST.

> **STE:** IF THE LOW PRESSURE CONDITION CONTINUES, REPLACE THE UNIT.

#### CONTINUOUS (adj) — APPROVED

- **Approved meaning:** That continues

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THERE IS CONTINUOUS MOVEMENT OF THE PROBE.

#### CONTINUOUSLY (adv) — APPROVED

- **Approved meaning:** In a continuous manner

> **STE:** APPLY PRESSURE CONTINUOUSLY.

#### CONTOUR (n) — APPROVED

- **Approved meaning:** The outer line of something

> **STE:** CUT ALONG THE CONTOUR TO REMOVE THE DAMAGED SKIN.

#### CONTROL (n) — APPROVED

- **Approved meaning:** Something that controls

> **STE:** IN AN EMERGENCY, USE THE MANUAL CONTROL.

#### CONTROL (v) — APPROVED

- **Approved meaning:** To give or send signals that adjust, operate, or keep something to a limit, or that cause something to operate
- **Forms:** CONTROLS, CONTROLLED, CONTROLLED

> **STE:** RADIO MASTER SWITCH 1 CONTROLS THE BUS BAR.

#### COOL (adj) — APPROVED

- **Approved meaning:** Moderately cold

> **STE:** WHEN THE AREA IS SUFFICIENTLY COOL, POLISH THE SURFACE.

> **STE:** WHEN THE BRAKES ARE COOL, OPERATE THE PARKING BRAKE.
> **Non-STE:** Apply the parking brake when the brakes have cooled.

#### COPY (n) — APPROVED

- **Approved meaning:** An object made the same as another object

> **STE:** SEND FIVE COPIES OF THE DEFECT REPORT TO THE ENGINEERING AUTHORITY.

#### CORNER (n) — APPROVED

- **Approved meaning:** The point or the angle made when lines, sides, or edges touch

> **STE:** EXAMINE THE OPENING FOR CRACKS THAT START AT ITS CORNERS.

#### CORRECT (adj) — APPROVED

- **Approved meaning:** That agrees with all that is necessary to the maximum

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE POLARITIES ARE CORRECT.

#### CORRECT (v) — APPROVED

- **Approved meaning:** To make correct
- **Forms:** CORRECTS, CORRECTED, CORRECTED

> **STE:** CORRECT THE TENSION.

#### CORRECTION (n) — APPROVED

- **Approved meaning:** A change to make something correct

> **STE:** DO NOT MAKE THE CORRECTION AT THIS HEADING.

#### CORRECTLY (adv) — APPROVED

- **Approved meaning:** In a correct manner

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE CIRCLIP IS CORRECTLY INSTALLED.

## Unapproved Words

#### contaminant (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Alternative:** CONTAMINATION (n)

> **STE:** THIS FILTER REMOVES ALL CONTAMINATION FROM THE AIR SUPPLY.
> **Non-STE:** This filter removes all contaminants from the air supply.

#### contaminated (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Alternative:** DIRTY (adj)

> **STE:** REPLACE THE DIRTY OIL.
> **Non-STE:** Contaminated oil must be replaced.

#### contiguous (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Alternative:** ADJACENT (adj)

> **STE:** REMOVE THE SENSOR TO GET ACCESS TO THE ADJACENT STRUCTURE.
> **Non-STE:** Remove the sensor to get access to the contiguous structure.

#### conventional (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Alternative:** STANDARD (adj)

> **STE:** PULL THE VEHICLE TO A SAFE AREA WHERE YOU CAN USE THE STANDARD PROCEDURES.
> **Non-STE:** Pull the vehicle to a safe location where more conventional procedures can be applied.

#### converse (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Alternative:** OPPOSITE (adj)

> **STE:** THE RESET DEVICE OPERATES IN THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION.
> **Non-STE:** The reset device operates in the converse direction.

#### convert (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Alternative:** CHANGE (v)

> **STE:** CHANGE THE UNITS FOR THE FUEL LOAD TO U.S. GALLONS.
> **Non-STE:** Convert the fuel load to U.S. gallons.

#### convey (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Alternative 1:** MOVE (v)

> **STE:** MOVE THE EQUIPMENT TO A SAFE AREA.
> **Non-STE:** Convey the equipment to a safe area.

- **Alternative 2:** TELL (v)

> **STE:** TELL THE INFORMATION TO YOUR MANAGER.
> **Non-STE:** Convey the information to your manager.

#### cool (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Alternative:** DECREASE (v)

> **STE:** LET THE TEMPERATURE OF THE COMPONENT DECREASE UNTIL IT IS THE SAME AS THE AMBIENT TEMPERATURE.
> **Non-STE:** Let the temperature of the component cool to ambient.

#### coordinate (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Alternative:** SYNCHRONIZED (adj)

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT COMPASS SYSTEM 1 AND COMPASS SYSTEM 2 ARE SYNCHRONIZED.
> **Non-STE:** Coordinate compass system 1 and compass system 2.

#### copy (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Alternative 1:** WRITE (v)

> **STE:** WRITE THE TAIL NUMBER ON THE RECORD FORM.
> **Non-STE:** Copy the tail number on the record form.

- **Alternative 2:** COPY (n)

> **STE:** MAKE A COPY OF THE DATA THAT IS ON THE IDENTIFICATION PLATE.
> **Non-STE:** Copy the data on the identification plate.

- **Alternative 3:** RECORD (v)

> **STE:** RECORD THE DATA THAT IS ON THE IDENTIFICATION PLATE.
> **Non-STE:** Copy the data on the identification plate.

#### correctness (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Alternative 1:** CORRECT (adj)

> **STE:** THIS ADJUSTMENT MUST BE CORRECT.
> **Non-STE:** The correctness of this adjustment is important.

- **Alternative 2:** CORRECTLY (adv)

> **STE:** IT IS IMPORTANT TO DO THIS ADJUSTMENT CORRECTLY.
> **Non-STE:** The correctness of this adjustment is important.

#### correspond (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Alternative 1:** AGREE (v)

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE POSITION OF THE CLAMP AGREES WITH THE POSITION THAT YOU RECORDED DURING THE REMOVAL PROCEDURE.
> **Non-STE:** Make sure that the position of the clamp corresponds to the position recorded during the removal procedure.

- **Alternative 2:** SAME (adj)

> **STE:** WHEN THE TWO GAUGES SHOW THE SAME INDICATION, CLOSE THE VALVE.

- **Alternative 3:** DIFFERENT (adj)

> **STE:** IF THE TWO INDICATIONS ARE DIFFERENT, DO THE TEST AGAIN.

#### corresponding (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Alternative:** RELATED (adj)

> **STE:** THE RELATED CAUTION LIGHT MUST GO OFF AFTER 30 SECONDS.
> **Non-STE:** The corresponding caution light shall go off after 30 seconds.

#### corrode (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Alternative:** CORROSION (TN)

> **STE:** THIS MATERIAL CAUSES CORROSION OF SURFACES WITH NO PROTECTION.
> **Non-STE:** This material corrodes unprotected surfaces.

#### corrosive (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Alternative:** CORROSION (TN)

> **STE:** HYDRAULIC FLUID CAN CAUSE CORROSION.
> **Non-STE:** Hydraulic fluid is corrosive.
