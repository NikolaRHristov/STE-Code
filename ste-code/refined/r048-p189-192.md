# ASD-STE100 — Dictionary (Check – Code)

> **Source:** ASD-STE100 Issue 9, January 2025
> **Pages:** 189–192 of 434

## Dictionary

### check (v) — UNAPPROVED

**Approved alternatives:**

- **MAKE SURE (v)**

> **STE:** Make sure that it is safe to supply electrical power.
> **Non-STE:** Check that it is safe to supply electrical power.

- **MEASURE (v)**

> **STE:** Measure the distance between the surfaces.
> **Non-STE:** Check the distance between the surfaces.

- **EXAMINE (v)**

> **STE:** Examine the casting for corrosion.
> **Non-STE:** Check the casting for corrosion.

- **CHECK (n)**

> **STE:** Do a leakage check of the valve.
> **Non-STE:** Check the valve for leakage.

### CHEMICAL (adj)

**Approved meaning:** Related to a chemical.

> **STE:** Remove the corrosion with an approved chemical compound.

### CHEMICALLY (adv)

**Approved meaning:** Related to a chemical.

> **STE:** Remove corrosion chemically.

### CLEAN (adj)

**Approved meaning:** That does not include dirt or unwanted material.

> **STE:** Make sure that the area is clean.

### CLEAN (v)

**Approved meaning:** To remove dirt or unwanted materials.

**Forms:** cleans, cleaned, cleaned

> **STE:** Clean the gasket contact surface.

### cleanliness (n) — UNAPPROVED

**Approved alternative:**

- **CLEAN (adj)**

> **STE:** Conditions for a satisfactory bond are:
> - A clean surface
> - A correct temperature
> - A correct pressure
>
> **Non-STE:** A satisfactory bond depends on the cleanliness of the surface, the temperature, and the pressure.

### CLEAR (adj)

**Approved meanings:**

1. Without blockage or interference.

> **STE:** Make sure that the pitot air inlet is clear.

2. Easy to understand and without ambiguity.

> **STE:** Always give clear instructions about safety.

For other meanings, use:

- **TRANSPARENT (adj)**

> **STE:** Put the unit in a transparent plastic bag.
> **Non-STE:** Put the unit in a clear plastic bag.

### clear (v) — UNAPPROVED

**Approved alternative:**

- **CLEAN (v)**

> **Non-STE:** Clear the drain hole.

> **Non-STE:** Lift the seat so that it clears the track locks.
