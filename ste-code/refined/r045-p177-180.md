# ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English

## Part 2 — Dictionary

### B

#### beside (prep) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** ADJACENT TO

> **STE:** THE FUEL PUMP IS ADJACENT TO THE STRUCTURAL SUPPORT.
> **Non-STE:** The fuel pump is beside the structural support.

#### BETWEEN (prep)

- **Approved meaning:** Related to something before and after in time or position

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE STOPWATCH INDICATION IS BETWEEN 2 AND 4 MINUTES.

#### beware (v) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** CAREFUL (adj)

> **STE:** BE CAREFUL OF DANGEROUS VOLTAGES.
> **Non-STE:** Beware of dangerous voltages.

#### beyond (prep) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** MORE THAN

> **STE:** REPLACE ALL COMPONENTS THAT ARE WORN MORE THAN THE MAXIMUM LIMITS.
> **Non-STE:** Replace all components that are worn beyond their maximum limits.

#### big (adj) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** LARGE (adj)

> **STE:** INSTALL THE LARGER BOLTS.
> **Non-STE:** Install the bigger bolts.

*If it is possible, give an accurate value.*

#### bind (v) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** FREELY (adv)

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE VALVES MOVE FREELY.
> **Non-STE:** Check that valves do not bind.

- **Approved alternative:** WIND AROUND

> **STE:** WIND TWO LAYERS OF TAPE AROUND THE PIPE JOINTS.
> **Non-STE:** Bind the pipe joints with two layers of tape.

#### blank (v) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** SEAL (v)

> **STE:** SEAL THE HOSE.
> **Non-STE:** Blank the hose.

- **Approved alternative:** CAP (TN)

> **STE:** PUT A CAP ON THE CONNECTOR.
> **Non-STE:** Blank the connector.

- **Approved alternative:** COVER (TN)

> **STE:** PUT A COVER ON THE OUTLET PORT.
> **Non-STE:** Blank the outlet port.

#### BLEED (v)

- **Forms:** BLEEDS, BLED, BLED
- **Approved meaning:** To let a gas out of

> **STE:** BLEED THE SPEEDBRAKE HYDRAULIC SYSTEM.

#### blend (v) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** MIX (v)

> **STE:** MIX THE ADHESIVE WITH THE HARDENER.
> **Non-STE:** Blend the adhesive with the hardener.

#### block (v) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** CAUSE A BLOCKAGE

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE SEALANT DOES NOT CAUSE A BLOCKAGE OF THE DRAIN.
> **Non-STE:** Check that the sealant does not block the drain.

#### BLOCKAGE (n)

- **Approved meaning:** That which prevents correct operation

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THERE IS NO BLOCKAGE OF THE PIPE.

#### BLOCKED (adj)

- **Approved meaning:** That prevents correct operation

> **STE:** A BLOCKED OIL JET CAN CAUSE INCORRECT OPERATION.

#### blot (v) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** DRY (v)

> **STE:** DRY THE SURFACE WITH A TOWEL.
> **Non-STE:** Blot the surface with a towel.

#### BLOW (v)

- **Forms:** BLOWS, BLEW, BLOWN
- **Approved meaning:** To cause gas to move

> **STE:** BLOW DRY AIR THROUGH THE FEED TUBES.

#### BLUNT (adj)

- **Approved meaning:** Not sharp

> **STE:** REMOVE THE SEALANT WITH A BLUNT SCRAPER.

#### blunted (adj) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** BLUNT (adj)

> **STE:** EXAMINE THE STATIC DISCHARGERS FOR BENT AND BLUNT PINS.
> **Non-STE:** Inspect the static dischargers for bent and blunted pins.

#### bolt (v) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** ATTACH (v) (WITH A BOLT [TN] OR BOLTS [TN])

> **STE:** ATTACH THE TRACK TO THE CHANNELS WITH THE BOLTS.
> **Non-STE:** Bolt the track to the channels.

#### BOND (n)

- **Approved meaning 1:** A connection between structural components for electrical continuity

> **STE:** DO A RESISTANCE CHECK OF THE PRIMARY BONDS.

- **Approved meaning 2:** A firm connection with another material

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE BOND BETWEEN THE HONEYCOMB CORE AND THE SKIN IS CORRECT.

#### BOND (v)

- **Forms:** BONDS, BONDED, BONDED
- **Approved meaning 1:** To make an electrical bond

> **STE:** THE STATIC DISCHARGER IS ELECTRICALLY BONDED TO THE FRAME.

- **Approved meaning 2:** To attach firmly or become firmly attached with another material

> **STE:** IF THE FILLER BONDS TO THE TOOL, MAKE THE TOOL MOIST WITH COLD WATER.

> **STE:** WHEN YOU BOND THE MATERIALS, APPLY A THIRD LAYER OF ADHESIVE.

#### both (adj) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** THE TWO (TN)

> **STE:** ATTACH THE TWO ENDS OF THE HOSE.
> **Non-STE:** Attach both ends of the hose.

#### BOTTOM (adj)

- **Approved meaning:** That is lowermost

> **STE:** REMOVE THE BOTTOM BOLT.

#### BOTTOM (n)

- **Approved meaning:** The lowermost position

> **STE:** THE DRAIN PLUG IS AT THE BOTTOM OF THE UNIT.

#### bottom (v) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** TOUCH (v)

> **STE:** OPEN THE DOOR UNTIL IT TOUCHES THE FLOOR.
> **Non-STE:** Open the door until it bottoms on the floor.

#### bounds (n) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** LIMIT (n)

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE CLEARANCE IS IN THE LIMITS.
> **Non-STE:** Make sure that the clearance is in bounds.

#### brace (v) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** HOLD (v)

> **STE:** SUPPORT STRUTS HOLD THE FLOOR STRUCTURE IN POSITION.
> **Non-STE:** Support struts brace the floor structure.

- **Approved alternative:** STRONG (adj) (verb form: BRACE [v])

> **STE:** REINFORCEMENT PLATES MAKE THE LAP JOINTS STRONGER.
> **Non-STE:** Reinforcement plates brace the lap joints.

#### branch (v) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** DIVIDE (v)

> **STE:** THE PRESSURE LINES DIVIDE IN DIFFERENT DIRECTIONS.
> **Non-STE:** The pressure lines branch in different directions.

#### breadth (n) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** WIDTH (n)

> **STE:** THE WIDTH OF THE MATERIAL MUST BE LARGER THAN 10 mm.
> **Non-STE:** The breadth of the material must be larger than 10 mm.

#### break (n) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** STOP (v)

> **STE:** IF THE TRANSMISSION STOPS, CANCEL THE TEST.
> **Non-STE:** If there is a break in the transmission, cancel the test.

#### BREAK (v)

- **Forms:** BREAKS, BROKE, BROKEN
- **Approved meaning:** To cause to separate or become separated into parts by force

> **STE:** BREAK THE BOND OF THE SEALANT.

> **STE:** IF THE SHEAR PIN BREAKS, DO AN INSPECTION OF THE TOWBAR.

#### breakdown (n) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** STOP (v)

> **STE:** IF THE PRESSURE SUPPLY STOPS, CANCEL THE TEST.
> **Non-STE:** If there is a breakdown in the pressure supply, cancel the test.

#### BREATHE (v)

- **Forms:** BREATHES, BREATHED, BREATHED
- **Approved meaning:** To get gas into or out of the lungs

> **STE:** DO NOT BREATHE THE SOLVENT FUMES.

> **STE:** BREATHE INTO THE OXYGEN MASK.

#### BRIGHT (adj)

- **Forms:** BRIGHTER, BRIGHTEST
- **Approved meaning:** That gives much light

> **STE:** THE NAVIGATION LIGHTS CHANGE FROM DIM TO BRIGHT.

#### BRIGHTLY (adv)

- **Approved meaning:** In a bright manner

> **STE:** THE LIGHTS COME ON BRIGHTLY.

#### brilliance (n) — UNNAPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** INTENSITY (n)

> **STE:** SET THE INTENSITY TO THE MINIMUM.
> **Non-STE:** Set the brilliance to the minimum.

- **Approved alternative:** BRIGHT (adj)

> **STE:** TURN THE BRT CONTROL CLOCKWISE TO MAKE THE DISPLAY BRIGHTER.
> **Non-STE:** Turn the BRT CONTROL clockwise to increase the display brilliance.
