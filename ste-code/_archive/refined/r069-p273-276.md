## Approved Words

#### HYDRAULIC (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Related to, or operated by, pressurized liquid.

> **STE:** CONNECT THE FITTINGS TO THE HYDRAULIC TEST BENCH.

#### HYDRAULICALLY (adv) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** With hydraulic power.

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE LINES ARE HYDRAULICALLY OPERATED.

#### IDENTIFICATION (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Something that identifies an object.

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE NAMEPLATE SHOWS THE CORRECT IDENTIFICATION OF THE UNIT.

#### IDENTIFY (v) — APPROVED

- **Forms:** IDENTIFIES, IDENTIFIED, IDENTIFIED
- **Meaning:**
  1. To use a specified code or marking to supply with an identity.
  2. To show an identity.

> **STE:** IDENTIFY THE WIRES WITH SLEEVES OF DIFFERENT COLORS.

> **STE:** A 406 MHz DIGITAL SIGNAL IDENTIFIES THE EMERGENCY LOCATOR TRANSMITTER AND ITS LOCATION.

- **Note:** For other meanings, use FIND (v).

> **STE:** FIND THE DAMAGED AREAS.
> **Non-STE:** Identify the damaged areas.

#### IF (conj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** In the event that, on the condition that, in case of.

> **STE:** IF YOU USE A REPLACEMENT FAIRING PLATE, CUT IT UNTIL YOU GET THE CORRECT MATING DIMENSION.

#### IGNORE (v) — APPROVED

- **Forms:** IGNORES, IGNORED, IGNORED
- **Meaning:** Not to think about something, not to do something about.

> **STE:** IGNORE THE VIBRATION INDICATIONS.

> **STE:** SELECT "CANCEL" TO IGNORE ALL CHANGES.

#### ILLUMINATE (v) — APPROVED

- **Forms:** ILLUMINATES, ILLUMINATED, ILLUMINATED
- **Meaning:** To make something visible with an external light source.

> **STE:** THE EMERGENCY LIGHTS COME ON TO ILLUMINATE THE EVACUATION PATH.

- **Note:** For lights or lamps, use COME ON or BE ON.

> **STE:** WHEN THE PRESSURE LIGHT COMES ON, READ THE VALUE ON THE PRESSURE GAUGE.
> **Non-STE:** When the pressure light illuminates, read the value on the pressure gauge.

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE LAMP COMES ON.
> **Non-STE:** Ensure the lamp illuminates.

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE LAMP IS ON.
> **Non-STE:** Ensure the lamp is illuminated.

#### IMMEDIATELY (adv) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Without a gap of time or space.

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE EMERGENCY LIGHT IMMEDIATELY GOES BACK TO "OFF."

## Unapproved Words

#### huge (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** LARGE (adj)

> **STE:** TO REMOVE THE PAINT STRIPPER, FLUSH THE SURFACE WITH LARGE QUANTITIES OF WATER.
> **Non-STE:** To remove the paint stripper, flush the surface with huge quantities of water.

#### hum (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** HUM (TN)

> **STE:** ADJUST THE POTENTIOMETER UNTIL YOU DO NOT HEAR A HUM FROM THE LOUDSPEAKER.
> **Non-STE:** Adjust the potentiometer until the loudspeaker no longer hums.

#### hurt (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** INJURY (n)

> **STE:** BE CAREFUL WHEN YOU DO THIS PROCEDURE. IF THE UNIT FALLS, IT CAN CAUSE INJURY TO PERSONS.
> **Non-STE:** Be careful when you do this procedure. If the unit falls, it can hurt persons.

#### identical (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** SAME (adj)

> **STE:** THE SAME PROCEDURE IS APPLICABLE TO THE TWO VHF SETS.
> **Non-STE:** The procedure is identical for both VHF sets.

#### idle (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** IDLE (TN)

> **STE:** OPERATE THE ENGINE AT IDLE FOR 20 MINUTES.
> **Non-STE:** Idle the engine for 20 minutes.

#### ignite (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** IGNITION (TN)

> **STE:** FUEL GOES THROUGH THE ATOMIZERS INTO THE COMBUSTION CHAMBER, WHERE IGNITION OCCURS.
> **Non-STE:** Fuel passes through the atomizers into the combustion chamber, where ignition occurs.

#### immediate (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** IMMEDIATELY (adv)

> **See:** Dictionary entry for IMMEDIATELY (adv).

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE SPEEDBRAKE RETRACTS IMMEDIATELY.
> **Non-STE:** Check the speedbrake for immediate retraction.

#### immerse (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** SOAK (v)

> **STE:** SOAK THE FILTER IN THE CLEANING SOLUTION FOR 4 HOURS.
> **Non-STE:** Immerse the filter in the cleaning solution for 4 hours.

- **Approved alternative:** PUT... FULLY INTO

> **STE:** PUT THE FILTER FULLY INTO THE SOLVENT.
> **Non-STE:** Immerse the filter in solvent.

#### immobilize (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** LOCK (v)

> **STE:** LOCK THE PULLEY WITH THE RIGGING PIN.
> **Non-STE:** Immobilize the pulley with the rigging pin.

- **Approved alternative:** PREVENT MOVEMENT

> **STE:** WHEN YOU REPAIR THE UNIT, PREVENT MOVEMENT OF THE ASSEMBLIES.
> **Non-STE:** Immobilize the assemblies when repairing the unit.

#### impact (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** HIT (v)

> **See:** Dictionary entry for HIT (v).

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT METAL OBJECTS DO NOT HIT THE MATING SURFACE.
> **Non-STE:** Avoid impact between metal objects and the mating surface.
