## Dictionary Entries

#### COUNT (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To add the number of objects or occurrences to get a total
- **Forms:** COUNTS, COUNTED
- **Example:** COUNT THE NUMBER OF TREADS ON THE TIRE.

#### COUNTERCLOCKWISE (adv) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** In the opposite direction to clockwise
- **Example:** MAKE SURE THAT YOU TURN THE CONTROL KNOB COUNTERCLOCKWISE.

#### couple (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** CONNECT (v), ATTACH (v)

> **STE:** CONNECT THE FUELING NOZZLE TO THE AIRCRAFT.
> **Non-STE:** Couple the fueling nozzle to the aircraft.

> **STE:** ATTACH THE FUELING NOZZLE TO THE AIRCRAFT.
> **Non-STE:** Couple the fueling nozzle to the aircraft.

#### cover (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** INCLUDE (v), HAVE (v), COVER (TN)

> **STE:** THIS PROCEDURE INCLUDES THE INSTRUCTIONS TO REMOVE THE CONSTANT SPEED DRIVE (CSD).
> **Non-STE:** This procedure covers the removal of the Constant Speed Drive (CSD).

> **STE:** THE BOTTOM EDGE OF THE PANEL HAS CARPET ON IT.
> **Non-STE:** The bottom edge of the panel is covered with carpet.

> **STE:** PUT THE COVER ON THE CONTAINER.
> **Non-STE:** Cover the container.

#### crack (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** OPEN (v), CRACK (TN)

> **STE:** WHEN THE RELIEF VALVE OPENS, THE PRESSURE DECREASES.
> **Non-STE:** When the relief valve cracks open, the pressure decreases.

> **STE:** IF THERE ARE CRACKS IN THE CENTER PLY, REPLACE THE WINDSHIELD PANEL.
> **Non-STE:** If a center ply is cracked, replace the windshield panel.

#### create (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** MAKE (v), CAUSE (v)

> **STE:** CUT THE BRACKET TO MAKE A CLEARANCE.
> **Non-STE:** Cut the bracket to create a clearance.

> **STE:** VIBRATION CAN CAUSE CRACKS.
> **Non-STE:** Vibration can create cracks.

#### critical (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** VERY IMPORTANT, CAREFUL (adj)

> **STE:** THE CONDITION OF THE UNIT IS VERY IMPORTANT FOR ITS PERFORMANCE.
> **Non-STE:** The condition of the unit is critical to its performance.

> **STE:** MAKE A CAREFUL ESTIMATE OF THE DAMAGED AREA.
> **Non-STE:** Make a critical estimate of the damaged area.

#### cross (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** ACROSS (adv), CORRECTLY (adv)

> **STE:** DO NOT GO ACROSS THE SAFETY LINE.
> **Non-STE:** Do not cross the safety line.

> **STE:** CONNECT THE WIRES CORRECTLY.
> **Non-STE:** Do not cross the wires.

#### curvature (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** BEND (n), CURVE (n), RADIUS (TN)

> **STE:** THE BEND IN THE PIPE MUST HAVE A 30 mm RADIUS.
> **Non-STE:** The curvature of the pipe must have a 30 mm radius.

> **STE:** THE RADIUS OF THE CURVE MUST BE A MINIMUM OF 8 mm.
> **Non-STE:** The radius of the curvature must be a minimum of 8 mm.

> **STE:** BEND THE PIPE TO THE CORRECT RADIUS.
> **Non-STE:** Bend the pipe to the correct curvature.

#### CURVE (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** The shape of something which is bent but which has no angles
- **Example:** THE RADIUS OF THE CURVE MUST BE A MINIMUM OF 8 mm.

#### curve (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** MAKE A CURVE

> **STE:** BEND THE STRIP OF METAL TO MAKE A CURVE.
> **Non-STE:** Curve the strip of metal.

#### CUT (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning 1:** To divide into parts
- **Meaning 2:** To remove with a sharp tool
- **Forms:** CUTS, CUT
- **Example 1:** CUT THE CABLE INTO THREE EQUAL LENGTHS.
- **Example 2:** CUT AND DISCARD THE LOCKWIRE.

#### CYCLE (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** One complete sequence of operation
- **Example:** SET THE SWITCH TO THREE CYCLES A MINUTE.

#### cycle (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** CYCLE (n)

> **STE:** OPERATE THE ACTUATOR THROUGH THREE CYCLES.
> **Non-STE:** Cycle the actuator three times.

#### DAMAGE (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** The result of an occurrence that causes deterioration of the condition of something
- **Example:** EXAMINE THE SURFACE FOR DAMAGE.

#### damage (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** DAMAGE (n)

> **STE:** DISCONNECT THE SPRING TO PREVENT DAMAGE TO THE ROD.
> **Non-STE:** Disconnect the spring so as not to damage the rod.

#### DAMAGED (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** That has or can be affected by damage
- **Example:** REPLACE THE DAMAGED PARTS.

#### damp (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** MOIST (adj)

> **STE:** REMOVE ALL DIRT FROM THE SEAT WITH A MOIST CLOTH.
> **Non-STE:** Remove all dirt from the seat with a damp cloth.

#### dampen (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** DECREASE (v), MAKE MOIST

> **STE:** THE MOUNTING PADS DECREASE VIBRATION.
> **Non-STE:** The mounting pads dampen vibration.

> **STE:** MAKE THE CLOTH MOIST WITH SOLVENT.
> **Non-STE:** Dampen the cloth with solvent.

#### danger (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** RISK (n)

> **STE:** THERE IS A RISK OF FIRE.
> **Non-STE:** There is a danger of fire.

#### DANGEROUS (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** That can cause injury, damage to health or can kill
- **Note:** "danger" is a technical noun when it identifies a safety instruction. Refer to section 7.

> **STE:** HYDRAULIC FLUID IS DANGEROUS FOR YOUR HEALTH.
> **Non-STE:** Hydraulic fluid is a danger to your health.

> **STE:** IT IS DANGEROUS TO USE AIR IN THIS SYSTEM.

#### dangerously (adv) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** DANGEROUS (adj)

> **STE:** DO NOT OPEN THE CARGO DOOR IN STRONG WINDS. STRONG WINDS ARE DANGEROUS.
> **Non-STE:** Do not open the cargo door if the wind-speed is dangerously high.

#### DATA (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning 1:** Known facts
- **Meaning 2:** Numbers and symbols used by computers
- **Note:** You can also use the singular.
- **Example 1:** MAKE SURE THAT YOU GET ALL THE DATA ABOUT THE PROBLEM.
- **Example 2:** DO A TEST TO FIND IF THE MANAGEMENT UNIT TRANSMITS THESE DATA.
- **Example 2 (singular):** DO A TEST TO FIND IF THE MANAGEMENT UNIT TRANSMITS THIS DATA.

#### DATE (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** The time at which an event occurs
- **Example:** ON THE LABEL, READ THE DATE OF THE LAST INSPECTION.

#### DEACTIVATE (v) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** To make a system, function, or feature no longer ready for operation
- **Example:** SET THE SWITCH TO THE STANDBY POSITION TO DEACTIVATE THE MONITORING BOX.
- **For other meanings, use:**
  - **STOP (v)**

> **STE:** STOP THE MOTOR.
> **Non-STE:** Deactivate the motor.

#### deadly (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** KILL (v)

> **STE:** HIGH VOLTAGES CAN KILL YOU.
> **Non-STE:** High voltages are deadly.

#### deaerate (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** BLEED (v), AIR (TN)

> **STE:** BLEED THE No. 1 HYDRAULIC SYSTEM.
> **Non-STE:** Deaerate the No. 1 hydraulic system.

> **STE:** THE VACUUM EQUIPMENT REMOVES THE AIR FROM THE COMPOUND.
> **Non-STE:** The vacuum equipment deaerates the compound.

#### dearm (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternatives:** DISARM (v), REMOVE (v)

> **STE:** DISARM THE ESCAPE SLIDE.
> **Non-STE:** Dearm the escape slide.

> **STE:** REMOVE THE CARTRIDGES FROM THE FIRE BOTTLES.
> **Non-STE:** Dearm the fire bottles.
