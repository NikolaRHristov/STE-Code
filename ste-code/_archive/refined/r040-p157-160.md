## Dictionary

### A

#### admit (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** LET (v)

> **STE:** OPEN THE VALVE TO LET NITROGEN GO INTO THE OLEO STRUT.
> **Non-STE:** Open the valve to admit nitrogen into the oleo strut.

#### adopt (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** USE (v)

> **STE:** IF THE UNIT IS DAMAGED, USE THIS PROCEDURE.
> **Non-STE:** Adopt this procedure if the unit is damaged.

#### advance (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** FORWARD (adj)

> **STE:** THE FORWARD MOVEMENT OF THE CONTROL LEVER MUST BE SLOW AND CONTINUOUS.
> **Non-STE:** The advance of the control lever must be slow and continuous.

#### advance (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** SET (v)

> **STE:** SET THE THROTTLE TO MAXIMUM POWER.
> **Non-STE:** Advance the throttle to maximum power.

#### FORWARD (adv) — APPROVED

> **STE:** MOVE THE LEVER FORWARD.
> **Non-STE:** Advance the lever.

#### adverse (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** BAD (adj)

> **STE:** REFER TO CHAPTER 6 FOR INSTRUCTIONS ABOUT HOW TO PARK IN BAD WEATHER CONDITIONS.
> **Non-STE:** For parking in adverse weather conditions, refer to Chapter 6.

*If it is possible, be accurate.*

> **STE:** THIS MEDICATION CAN CAUSE DERMATITIS.
> **Non-STE:** This medication can cause adverse reactions.

#### advisable (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** RECOMMEND (v)

> **STE:** THE DESIGN AUTHORITY RECOMMENDS THAT YOU TORQUE THE BOLTS AGAIN AT INTERVALS OF SIX MONTHS.
> **Non-STE:** According to the Design Authority, it is advisable to retorque the bolts at intervals of six months.

#### advise (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** TELL (v)

> **STE:** TELL THE RAMP AGENT THAT THE BRAKES ARE SET.
> **Non-STE:** Advise the ramp agent that the brakes are set.

#### RECOMMEND (v) — APPROVED

> **STE:** THE SAFETY OFFICER RECOMMENDS THE APPLICABLE PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT.
> **Non-STE:** The safety officer advises on applicable personal protective equipment.

#### affect (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** EFFECT (n)

> **STE:** MAGNETIC TOOLS HAVE AN UNWANTED EFFECT ON THE COMPASS SYSTEM.
> **Non-STE:** Magnetic tools affect the compass system.

*If it is possible, be accurate.*

> **STE:** THIS MEDICATION CAN CAUSE DERMATITIS.
> **Non-STE:** This medication can affect your skin.

#### AFT (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** At or nearer to the rear of an air or sea vehicle.

> **STE:** THE PUMP IS IN THE AFT CELL OF THE FUSELAGE TANK.

#### AFT (adv) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** In the direction of the rear of an air or sea vehicle.

> **STE:** MOVE THE THROTTLE AFT.

#### AFTER (conj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** That follows a specified time, sequence, or operation.

> **STE:** AFTER YOU INSTALL THE COMPONENT, DO A FUNCTIONAL TEST.

#### AFTER (prep) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** That follows a specified time, sequence, or operation.

> **STE:** THE BAR MOVES DOWN AFTER 20 SECONDS.

#### AFT OF (prep) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** At a position nearer to the rear.

> **STE:** THE CONTROL UNIT IS INSTALLED AFT OF THE FLIGHT COMPARTMENT.

#### AGAIN (adv) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** One more occurrence.

> **STE:** MOVE THE CONTROL STICK BACK TO THE CENTER, AND THEN MOVE IT FORWARD AGAIN.

#### AGAINST (prep) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** In contact with.

> **STE:** PUT THE ADAPTER IN POSITION AGAINST ITS SUPPORT.

#### AGENT (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** One of a group of materials made to do a specified task.

> **STE:** DO NOT USE THESE CLEANING AGENTS ON HOT SURFACES.

#### aggravate (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** INCREASE (v)

> **STE:** TIRE WEAR INCREASES WITH HIGH SPEED.
> **Non-STE:** Tire wear is aggravated by high speed.

#### agitate (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** SHAKE (v)

> **STE:** SHAKE THE SOLUTION.
> **Non-STE:** Agitate the solution.

#### agitation (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** SHAKE (v)

> **STE:** IF YOU SHAKE THE SOLUTION, DO NOT USE IT FOR ONE HOUR.
> **Non-STE:** After agitation, do not use the solution for one hour.

#### AGREE (v) — APPROVED

- **Forms:** AGREES, MUST AGREE WITH, AGREED
- **Meaning:** To be consistent with.

> **STE:** THE INDICATIONS MUST AGREE WITH THE VALUES IN TABLE 1001.

#### ahead (adv) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** FORWARD (adv)

> **STE:** WHEN YOU INSTALL THE ANTENNA, THE ALIGNMENT ARROW MUST POINT FORWARD.
> **Non-STE:** When you install the antenna, the alignment arrow must point ahead.

#### AID (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Help that is given.

> **STE:** IF YOU GET THIS CHEMICAL SOLUTION INTO YOUR EYES, GET MEDICAL AID IMMEDIATELY.

#### aid (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** HELP (v)

> **STE:** APPLY SAND TO THE PLANKS TO HELP WITH TIRE TRACTION.
> **Non-STE:** Apply sand to the planks to aid with tire traction.

#### aim (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** POINT (v)

> **STE:** DO NOT POINT THE HOSE AT PERSONS.
> **Non-STE:** Do not aim the hose at persons.

#### AIRBORNE (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** In, or used in, flight.

> **STE:** POSITION 2 OF THE SWITCH GIVES A SIGNAL OF THE AIRCRAFT AIRBORNE CONDITION.

#### air-dry (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** DRY (v)

> **STE:** DRY THE FILTER IN THE AIR.
> **Non-STE:** Air-dry the filter.

#### AIRFLOW (n) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** A flow of air.

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE AIRFLOW IS NOT MORE THAN 10 LITERS/MINUTE.

#### alert (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** TELL (v)

> **STE:** TELL ALL PERSONS TO GO AWAY FROM THE AREA.
> **Non-STE:** Alert all persons to go away from the area.

#### ALIGN (v) — APPROVED

- **Forms:** ALIGNS, ALIGNED
- **Meaning:** To put or come into a specified position in relation to a line.

> **STE:** ALIGN THE FLANGE HOLES WITH THE STUDS.

> **STE:** ALL THREE BRACKET HOLES MUST ALIGN WITH THE FUEL TUBE.

#### alignment (n) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** ALIGN (v)

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE KEYWAY IS AT THE TOP AND ALIGNS WITH THE PEG.
> **Non-STE:** Ensure that the keyway is at the top and in alignment with the peg.

#### ALL (adj) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Full quantity.

> **STE:** DRAIN ALL THE FLUIDS.

#### allocate (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** GIVE (v)

> **STE:** GIVE THE DIFFERENT MAINTENANCE TASKS TO THE APPROVED TECHNICIANS.
> **Non-STE:** Allocate the different maintenance tasks to the approved technicians.

#### allow (v) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** LET (v)

> **STE:** DO NOT LET THE CABLE TOUCH THE FLOOR.
> **Non-STE:** Do not allow the cable to touch the floor.

#### allowable (adj) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** PERMITTED (adj)

> **STE:** THE MAXIMUM PERMITTED DIFFERENCE IS 5 mm.
> **Non-STE:** The maximum allowable difference is 5 mm.

#### APPROVED (adj) — APPROVED

> **STE:** THE VALUES MUST NOT BE MORE THAN THE APPROVED DIFFERENCES GIVEN IN TABLE 1.
> **Non-STE:** The values must not be more than the allowable differences given in table 1.

#### ALMOST (adv) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** Near the limit or the value.

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE PRESSURE IN THE CABIN IS ALMOST ZERO.

#### ALONG (prep) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** In a line parallel with the length or direction of.

> **STE:** MOVE THE TENSIOMETER ALONG THE CABLE.

#### already (adv) — UNAPPROVED

- **Approved alternative:** IN PROGRESS (adv)

> **STE:** THE DATABASE SYNCHRONIZATION IS IN PROGRESS.
> **Non-STE:** The database is already synchronizing.

> **STE:** YOU CAN DO THIS REPAIR PROCEDURE ONLY IF THERE IS NO OTHER DAMAGE.
> **Non-STE:** Only do this repair procedure if there is not already damage.

*Frequently, an alternative for this word is not necessary.*

> **STE:** MAKE SURE THAT THE SOFTWARE IS INSTALLED IN THE REPOSITORY.
> **Non-STE:** Make sure that the software is already installed in the repository.

#### ALSO (adv) — APPROVED

- **Meaning:** In addition.

> **STE:** ALSO DO THE APPLICABLE STEPS OF THIS PROCEDURE.
