| Run-on structure | All three notices crammed into one sentence with no visual/topical separation. | Split into one line per notice so each signal word governs a distinct thought. |
| Ambiguous antecedent | "your limit" could refer to the request-per-minute cap or a broader account ceiling. | Clarified to "rate limit" to match the previously mentioned threshold. |
| Passive-voice padding | "will be temporarily disabled" reads weaker than needed for a WARNING. | Tightened to "will result in temporary API key disablement" — still passive but sharper; the consequence leads the clause. |
| Missing actionable path | The NOTE ended on "contacting support" with no clue what to ask for. | Explicit: "increase your rate limit." |

Signal-word hierarchy used: **WARNING** (operational consequence with enforcement action) → **IMPORTANT** (service-level advisory, non-punitive) → **NOTE** (supplemental, user-controlled).
