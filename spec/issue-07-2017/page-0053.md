Sentences
## Rule 5.1 Write short sentences. Use a maximum of 20 words in each sentence.
Procedures contain instructions that tell you how to do a task. Long sentences are not easy to
understand.
In STE, the maximum length of a sentence for procedures is 20 words. (Refer to section 8 for the
rules about word count.)
Warnings, cautions, and other safety instructions must also obey this rule.
Examples in STE:
Remove the three auxiliary screws (22) from the flange of the motor assembly (9).
(This sentence has 14 words.)
### WARNING: WHEN YOU REMOVE THE SHROUD (26), BE CAREFUL NOT TO
### CAUSE DAMAGE TO THE FLANGE ASSEMBLY (22).
(This sentence has 17 words.)
