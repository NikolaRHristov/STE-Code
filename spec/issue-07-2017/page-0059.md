Content structure
Descriptive writing gives information, not instructions. Descriptive writing can be:
- A description of an item, a product, a system, or a component, its function, how it is made
  and how it operates
- A text that gives general information
- A note in a procedure.
## Rule 6.1 Give information gradually.
In a descriptive text, give information gradually and make sure that each sentence contains only
one topic. If you give too much information too quickly, your text will be difficult to understand and it
will be necessary for the reader to read it again.
