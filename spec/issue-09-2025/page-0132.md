Use an approved word only as the specified part of speech. Refer to part 1, section 1. In the dictionary, there are eight parts of speech as follows: 1. noun (n): A word that is the name of a person, place, object, idea, quality, or activity. 2. verb (v): A word that shows a state of being or an action. Its tense (present, past, future) tells you when the action occurs. 3. adjective (adj): A word that gives details about a noun, noun phrase, or multi-word noun. It is related to the type, size, color, or number of the noun, noun phrase, or multi-word noun. Adjectives can be of a qualitative and a quantitative type and can have comparative and superlative forms. Their position in the sentence can be before nouns or after verbs. 4. adverb (adv): A word that modifies a verb, an adjective, or a different adverb. It gives an answer to the questions: “how,” “where,” “when,” “how often,” and “how much.” |
