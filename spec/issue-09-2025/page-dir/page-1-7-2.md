**ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English** 


#### **Rule 7.2 Start a safety instruction with a clear and accurate command or condition.** 

Start a safety instruction with a clear and accurate command or condition. Your reader must know how to prevent accidents and keep a high level of safety. 

##### **Examples in STE:** 

(Refer to the underlined command.) 

<u>WARNING: DO NOT SWALLOW THE SOLVENT. ALWAYS MAKE SURE THAT YOU KNOW</u> THE SAFETY PRECAUTIONS AND FIRST AID INSTRUCTIONS FOR SOLVENTS. SOLVENTS ARE POISONOUS AND CAN CAUSE INJURY OR DEATH. 

<u>CAUTION: DO NOT USE BLEACH OR CLEANSERS THAT CONTAIN CHLORINE TO CLEAN</u> THE UNIT. THESE CLEANING AGENTS CAN CAUSE CORROSION. 

If your reader must know about a condition before the start of a procedure or work step, give this condition first. 

##### **Examples in STE:** 

(Refer to the underlined condition.) 

<u>WARNING: WHILE YOU USE THE SPRAY PAINT, POINT THE SPRAY AWAY FROM YOUR</u> FACE. THE SPRAY PAINT CAN CAUSE INJURY TO YOUR EYES. 

<u>CAUTION: WHEN YOU ASSEMBLE THE UNIT, DO NOT LET THE PARTS FALL.</u> IF THEY FALL, PERMANENT DAMAGE TO THE PARTS CAN OCCUR.
