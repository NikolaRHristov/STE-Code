**ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English** 


<u>Words that have an “-ing” form and are technical nouns or parts of technical nouns</u> 

You can use a word that has an “-ing” form as a technical noun (for example, in procedural titles or headings). 

##### **Examples:** 

<mark>Cleaning, Testing and Fault Isolation, Handling, Packaging, Shipping, Troubleshooting</mark> 

You can also use the “-ing” form of a verb as a modifier in a technical noun. This modifier is an adjective that is related to the function of a system, component, part, tool, material, or equipment. 

##### **Examples:** 

<mark>Air-conditioning system, degreasing agent, grinding wheel, polishing disc, sanding machine, switching relay, welding torch</mark> 

<u>Approved words that have an “-ing” form</u> 

Only a small number of approved words in the dictionary have an “-ing” form. They are: 

- Nouns (lighting, opening, routing, and servicing) 

- Adjectives (mating, missing, and remaining) 

- A pronoun (something) 

- A preposition (during). 

### **Active voice** 

#### **Rule 3.6 Use the active voice. In descriptive writing, you can use the passive voice only if the agent is unknown.** 

Technical texts consist of procedural writing and descriptive writing. When you write in STE, always <u>use the active voice. In descriptive writing, the passive voice is permitted only when the agent (the</u> person or thing that does the action) is unknown. 

##### <u>What is active voice?</u> 

In the active voice, the subject of the sentence does the action of the sentence (“A” does “B”). Thus, the grammatical subject (A) is also the logical subject (agent). 

##### <u>What is passive voice?</u> 

In the passive voice, the subject of the sentence receives the action (“B” is done by “A”). Here, the grammatical subject is B, and the logical subject, or agent, is A. 

##### **General examples** : 

<mark>Active: The manufacturer gives the safety procedures.</mark> _<mark>Passive: The safety procedures are given by the manufacturer.</mark>_ <mark>Active: The side stay holds the main gear leg.</mark> _<mark>Passive: The main gear leg is held by the side stay.</mark>_
