### **List of recurring errors** 

The table that follows gives you a list of the most frequently recurring errors that writers make when they use STE. If a word is not approved in the dictionary, do not use it.

|---|---|
|acceptable(adj)|PERMITTED(adj)|
|alternate(adj)|ALTERNATIVE(adj)|
