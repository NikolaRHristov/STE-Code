What is ASD-STE100?
• “ASD Simplified Technical English (STE) is an international
  specification for the preparation of technical
  documentation in a controlled language.”
• Set of writing rules
• Dictionary with approved and non-approved terms
• STE continues to be relevant 35 years after it was designed to
  simplify maintenance documentation in aerospace.
• Majority of companies requesting the (free) specification are
  outside aerospace and defense.
• Request your copy at ASD-STE100.org.
